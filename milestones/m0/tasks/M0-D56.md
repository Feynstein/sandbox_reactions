# M0-D56 — P5 on the GPU at heat_spot's first step: the cause measured, the solver fixed

Box: win-laptop (Laser2025-20, RTX 4080 Laptop), 2026-10-18. Model: claude-opus-5-5, level high (`rung_record.py now`).
Every tolerance of §0.5 [M0-TJ4] (3)–(4), the ceiling of [M0-D37] and G-FLD's check are unchanged.

## The cause, measured per pass
Probe: a temporary test, `heat::limiter::probe_d56_spot`, and a temporary per-slot log in heat.wgsl (`PROBE-D56`). Both
are removed. The output is in logs/M0-D56.probe.log. It covers heat_spot.json at Δt 0.05 and a_r 4e-5, steps 0–2. For
each pass it logs: the end test's two sums, the true residual's u-measure ÷ its bound at the solve's start, the rounds,
and both measures ÷ their bounds where the solve ends.

**Shipped solver.** Step 0: 64 passes, 3,059 rounds, unconverged.
- From pass 6, every solve ran 43–53 rounds. It ended with the recursive γ at 0.01–0.04 × the u-bound, the heat measure
  being the binding one (0.4–1.0 × its bound).
- The next pass's true residual read **37–90 × the u-bound**. So the rounds' r had drifted about 10³ from b − A x.
- Σ C|ΔT| ÷ Σ C T⁰ stayed flat at 4–17 × 10⁻⁵ over passes 7–64, against the 10⁻⁶ test. No cell moved by more than 10⁻³
  after pass 6.
- The RESID slot's residual read the same in the shipped form b − (d x − Σ D x_j) and in the face form
  −(C/Δt)(T − T⁰) − Σ_f D_f (u_i − u_f) (pass 10: 36.9 against 37.1). So RESID's arithmetic was not the cause.

**Cause 1 — the products with A cancel in f32.** In the rounds, w = d·(r/d) − Σ_f D_f v_j, with d ≈ c + Σ_f D_f
≈ 1.3 × 10⁶ against c ≈ 3–19. On the slow, smooth modes A v ≈ c v, so the cancellation costs about ε d / c ≈ 2 % of A v
in each round.
- Fix candidate 1: every product formed face by face, c_i v_i + Σ_f D_f (v_i − v_f), with c_i taken from C_i and T.
- Result, step 0: 48 passes, 203 rounds, converged.
- But from pass 7, each solve ran **one** round. The next pass's true residual read **21.6 × the u-bound**, constant to
  three digits.
- Σ C|ΔT| crept down by about 5 % a pass (6.8 → 3.5 × 10⁻⁶ over passes 7–19) and met 10⁻⁶ only at pass 48.

**Cause 2 — u in one f32 word.** `CELL_UPDATE` → `build` reset x = u_of(T) each pass, with T in f32. The f32 resolution
of u, times D_f/c ≈ 10⁵, leaves b − A x at about 20 × (10⁻⁵)² Σ c u².
- So every pass was forced to run a round. Each forced round shrank a slow error mode, already inside the solve's
  tolerance, by about 5 %.
- Diagnostic only, not shipped: with the u-measure taken out of the stop test, step 0 ended in 7 passes and 161 rounds.
  Pass 7 ran 0 rounds, with Σ C|ΔT| at 6 × 10⁻⁹.

**The fix (shipped).** Candidate 1, plus the iterate's u carried in two f32 words from pass to pass:
- `raise` adds to x in two words (Fast2Sum): SV_L is the low word, in the plane the right side b used to hold. The
  residual is now face by face, so b is not needed.
- The two words are reset to u_of(T) wherever the factor-2 clamp or u_vac moved the cell.
- Step 0: **9 passes, 163 rounds, converged**. The true residual read 16.2 → 1.92 → 1.52 → 0.53 × the bound, pass 9 ran
  0 rounds, and Σ C|ΔT| was 0.
- Steps 1 and 2: 7 and 5 passes. The twin: 8 passes, 472 rounds.
