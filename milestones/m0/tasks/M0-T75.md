## Defaults taken (cheap to reverse, not in §4.12)
- Every decision is made on the rounded value: 999,999 K → "1.00 million K" (the task's example text shows "1,000,000 K" as
  the wrong answer to avoid), 999.6 ms → "1.00 seconds", 99,999 → "1.00e5", 0.0009996 → "0.00100".
- Grouping applies to every fixed number (mass, temperature, `actual`, inspector, integers); ladder rungs print as written
  (×1000, not ×1,000). Above 10⁹ K the billion value is grouped ("1,500 billion K"), same for trillions of years.
- Zero reads "0.00" (and "0.00 milliseconds"); NaN and infinities print as Rust writes them (a bug upstream, not a state).
- `rate` is `age` over years gained per wall second (§1.10.4).
