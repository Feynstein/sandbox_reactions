## Cause (measured)
- Where: « crashed after 30 checks · plants=18/18 » = section A's 11 checks + section B's 18 plants and 1 check, so
  the crash is section C's first `spans()` (`ran_ = {s[0] for s in spans()}`): 19 fixture scopes, each
  `{python} -S emit.py trace <name> 0`, all ending at once and appending a `<name> <start> <end>` line to one
  `trace.txt`.
- Why: on Windows an `open(…, "a")` append is a seek-to-end then a write, not one atomic step; two children can write
  at the same offset. A shorter line written over a longer one leaves its tail as a line of < 3 fields → the
  reader's `s.split()[2]` IndexError. A full overwrite loses a record instead.

  HEAD's writer **7 records lost**, 0 torn lines that run; the new writer **0 lost, 0 torn**. M0-D7's

- Reader (`spans()`): whole records only (`(\S+) (\d+\.\d+) (\d+\.\d+)`, fullmatch). A torn record reads as missing
