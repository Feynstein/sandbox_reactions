# M0-T57 — The ledger (detail)

Ran on: linux-pc (laserax-ai), RTX 5090, 2026-10-16; model=claude-sonnet-5-5 level=high (rung_record.py now).

## What exists
- `crates/sr-engine/src/ledger.rs` (new): `Ledger::{new, restart, update, start, last, json}`, `Record` {at, mass, energy, momentum} with
  `Mass/Energy/Momentum::invariant()` (§2.9's signs), `take_booked(device, queue, booking)` (book_totals over the booking, then clears the
  groups that hold booked terms; the three accumulator groups are left to the summary).
- `Step::summarise(device, queue) -> BlockSummary` (step/latch.rs, beside `poll_frames`): the block summary of the state as it stands, outside a
  frame — headless runs bare steps and the summary came only with frames. One submission; the accumulators it summed are cleared in the same
  submission (`encode_reset` right after `encode`), so the next summary holds what accrued after this one.
- `headless.rs`: the run's first step is a chunk of its own, then the ledger starts (`restart`) at the summary after it; every later chunk
  `take_booked` + `summarise` + `update`; summary.json's `ledger` = `Ledger::json()` (`--cpu-reference` keeps its empty start/end).
- tests/gpu/ledger.rs (4 cases), scope `ledger` in tools/pb/verify.json, plant tests/plants/ledger-no-escaped.patch, docs/agent/testing.md row.

## Declared readings (the contract leaves them open)
1. The ledger starts after the run's first step, not before step 0: the box is fitted and φ computed inside step 0, so no summary of the initial
   state exists. `start.step` = 1 (or the dump's step + 1 on `--load-dump`). Mass/momentum conservation is graded from there.
2. The summary covers the box; the outside is the floor state, so the grid mass adds `sigma_floor × (cells − box cells)`. The floor gas's energy
   outside the box is not added (~1e-9 of a star's). Checked: ledger grid mass and momentum vs an f64 sum of the final dump, 1e-6, every run.
3. Sinks' ½Mv² is in `kinetic` (§2.9 names none; M0-V7 observation 2). W = ½ΣmΦ over cells only — the sinks' own mφ is not in the summary.
   Nothing here runs sinks, so no case grades either: NOT PROVEN (no sink in any ledger run).
4. `cleared` is booked and listed, not in the mass invariant (§2.9's invariant omits it; a clear restarts the ledger).

## Findings not acted on
- Source-term gravity's momentum error (M0-V7 observation 1) is why the leave-with-gravity case is graded at 5e-3 and not 1e-6; the plan's
  "momentum (grid + sinks + escaped) to 1e-6" holds for the bounce run and (to what a vacuum reset removes) for the run without gravity. Not filed:
  the flag already carries it and the lead ruled on source-term gravity at M0-V7.
- The energy invariant drifts by 0.4–0.9 of its scale in these pressureless collapses (reported, not graded; G-CONS grades whole lives, M0-T106).
  Suspects: the cell-sized singular collapse (kinetic grows, W is the stale cadence's φ) — not measured, so not filed.
- The vacuum reset's unbooked momentum (§2.7 [M0-T19]) is 2.9e-4 of Σm|u| in the leaving run — the contract's own words, not a defect.

## The split (a second thread, not done)
`take_booked` reads the whole booking (23 planes, ~22 MB at 600 × 400) every chunk: fine for headless, not for the app's per-frame path.
The carried flag's (M0-T18) fixed-order GPU partial reduction of the booked groups in TERMS order (a shader and its scope) is the split;
the ledger's `update` already takes the totals as an array, so the reduction replaces `take_booked` alone.
