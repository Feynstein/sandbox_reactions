# M0-T51 — Sinks on the GPU (P3) — detail

Box: linux-pc (laserax-ai), RTX 5090 (the physics adapter); model=claude-opus-5-5 level=high (rung_record at launch).

## What exists
- `crates/sr-engine/shaders/sinks.wgsl` (new): `sink_candidates` (box tiles, 8 × 8; each cell tests §2.6's four criteria, φ =
  side.phi + the sinks' own −GM/√(r² + r_acc²); the tile's best by a fixed tree into `st.cand`) and `sink_update` (one
  workgroup of 256: the tiles' best by a strided scan + tree, formation, accretion sink by sink with a fixed-tree sum and a
  storage barrier between sinks, KDK, merging on invocation 0). No atomic.
- `crates/sr-engine/src/step/sinks.rs` (new): `Sinks` (the two dispatches, the state buffer: 8-word header, 16 × 8-word sinks,
  then 2 words per world tile), `SinkSetup { side }`, `SinkReport`, `Step::set_sinks`, `Step::read_sinks`.
- `step/mod.rs`: `Step::with_sinks` (`with_reactions` delegates with `None`); P3's slot filled before the latch is built, so a
  frame's latch reaches both dispatches (one indirect on the box's sizes, one fixed [1, 1, 1]).
- `tests/gpu/sink.rs`: 8 GPU cases (one ignored, M0-D44); `tests/plants/sink-momentum.patch` plants sinks.wgsl too;
  verify.json `sink` expected 23; testing.md's row.

## Readings declared (cheap to reverse)
1. M0-T50's readings, all kept (the carried flag). The cell a sink takes is left at Σ_floor *exactly* (the twin scales Σ by
   Σ_floor/Σ): a second sink's `Σ ≤ Σ_floor` test then refuses it, which is how a cell two sinks reach goes to the first.
2. A sink's mass is a pair of f32, m + m_lo, Fast2Sum and renormalised so m = fl(m + m_lo): thin gas accreting onto a heavy
   sink is not lost below m's last bit, and the pair's f64 sum splits back into the same pair (`set_sinks`) — M0-T52b's round
   trip can carry `m` as one f64.
3. A step without P2 binds zeros for g (tests' synthetic worlds, φ uploaded by hand); a step's P3 reads P2's g, the gas's alone
   today — as the twin's `advance` reads it.
4. Not wired into any runner: headless.rs and the app build no P3 (the scene's `sinks` switch is read nowhere yet). No block of
   lot 7 names that wiring; the conservation and ledger blocks will need it.
