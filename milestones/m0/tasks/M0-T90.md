## Decisions (cheap to reverse, declared)
- A refused drop (`NoRoom`) leaves the preset armed so the next click can try elsewhere; Esc or another button clears it and the line. A drop spends
  the click and clears both.
- A drop while paused asks the clock for one step (`Command::Step`, the Step button's own) — an edit lands at a step's P0 and a paused clock takes none,
  so the star would never show (M0-T84's flag). Running, the clock's steps carry it: up to ~0.45 s on an empty world at ×1 (not changed).
- The outline is the cloud's radius for the mass (`cloud_shape`), the engine's own — the shipped masses give 39, 56 and 93 cells, under the 150 cap; the test
  adds a 40 × M₀ mass (253 uncapped, 150 capped) so the cap is exercised.
