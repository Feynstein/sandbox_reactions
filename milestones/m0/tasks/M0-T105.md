## Readings declared (cheap to reverse; §2.12.5 does not say)
- A state trigger and a repeat cannot be combined (one of `step`, `object_until`, `mass_until`), so «at X_H,c = 0.5 until it weighs m» cannot be written as one trigger: every entry fires at X_H,c < 0.5 and the mass is the file's own (cells × frames), checked after the edit against the case's target (± 6 %). A calibration or physics change that moves the star at X_H,c 0.5 turns the band red — regenerate the file.
- «Erased from outside in» is the file's order (rings 100, 80, 62), not a mass trigger: a repeating `mass_until at_most` erase would repeat one disk and erase nothing more, and a second repeating entry never fires while the first is unspent (the backlog gate in `EditPlan::fire`).
- «The prediction after the edit» = the engine's `Predictor` on the mass SETTLE = 64 steps after the first frame whose mass moved > 1 % on the frame before (the edits land in ≤ 6 steps).
- The post-edit fate must be sure, one the case allows (supernova or black hole | white dwarf) and not the old one.
