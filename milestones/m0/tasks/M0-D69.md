## The cause
- Σ_i Σ_i g_i vanishes in exact arithmetic for central differences of φ = K ∗ Σ (K even: the pair terms are antisymmetric) —
  only if every difference reads the true φ. The twin and grav.wgsl fell back to a one-sided difference on the box's
  outermost ring. Inside the world the box's 8-cell margin keeps that ring empty; at a world edge the box is clamped and gas
  sits on it.
- The free-space φ one cell past the box is already in the zero-padded convolution: the padded size n ≥ 2w, so a target at
  x = −1 or x = w sees sources at offsets |d| ≤ w ≤ n/2, and K(n/2) = K(−n/2) (K even), so the one aliased offset is exact.

## Built
- Twin: `Convolver::convolve_ringed(field, w, h, ring)` (the padded result over the box grown by `ring`, exact for ring ≤ 1);
  `grav::acceleration` takes the ringed φ and differences centrally everywhere; `Gravity::field` crops φ back to the box.
- GPU: `grav_columns` keeps rows y = h and y = fft_h − 1 into the work buffer's rows h and h + 1; `grav_rows_inverse` (new,
  grav.wgsl; fft.wgsl's `conv_rows_inverse` stays P7's) runs h + 2 rows, writes the box's φ as before and keeps φ at x = w and
  x = fft_w − 1 per box row and the two ring rows (x < w) in the work buffer; `grav_gradient` reads them on the ring. The work
  buffer grows by two rows (8 · max_nx · (height + 2) bytes). Still 4 dispatches (5 with P3).
- Checks: grav_selfforce gains a twin case (the scatter with no empty rim, 72 ring cells with mass) and a GPU case (a 128 ×
  128 world with matter on all four edges, box the world, FFT 256²); plants `grav-ring-one-sided` (shader) and
  `grav-twin-ring-one-sided` (twin); `grav-gradient-backward` re-cut to the new gradient. The ledger's leave band 7e-3 (the
  lead) and `grav-ring-one-sided` as a ledger plant too.
