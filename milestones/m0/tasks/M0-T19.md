## Findings, measured
- First run: `eos_helpers…` red — ε_cold at Σ = 3.16e8 (x above the table's 1e8) GPU 4.755118e6 vs CPU 4.754868e6,
  5.3e-5 > 2e-5. Cause: extrapolation's slope ln u[511] − ln u[510] taken from two f32 values near 34 (≈ 4e-5 relative
  on the slope, × ~17 nodes past the end). Fixed in the implementation (slopes taken in f64 and uploaded), tolerance
  unchanged; then GO, worst ε_cold 5.1e-6.
- Worst errors (cold pairs on): 1/μ 6.3e-8, Y_e 4.1e-8, Z_met 5.0e-8, Π_e 3.3e-7, Π_n 2.4e-7, ε_cold 5.1e-6, Π 2.6e-7,
  c² 2.4e-7, T 1.3e-7. Field: vacuum E 3.6e-7, floored E 5.2e-6, fractions 6.9e-8; booked vs changed per cell and in
  total within 1e-6.
- The plant turns only the cold-cells case red: the booking case still passes under it (P8 books exactly what it
  changed, 0 for 0) — expected; the energy the floor should have added is graded against the twin.

## Not measured, from source (handed on)
- A NaN fraction: WGSL's `max(NaN, 0.0)` may give 0 or NaN; either way a NaN sum fails `sum > 0` and the cell becomes
  pure hydrogen, so P8 (before P9) can mask a NaN in the fractions from P9's non-finite guard. A NaN Σ or E passes P8
  untouched (`σ < Σ_vac` is false). Flagged to M0-T20.
- The headless Sun-like disk (`sun_disk_planes`) writes E = Σ·ε without ε_cold, so P8 now floors cells whose ε_cold
  exceeds that ε; not measured (boot checks only the summary's keys). For the preset/scene block when calibration lands.
