# M0-D37 — P5's Newton passes hit the 16-pass ceiling on sun_disk: the cause, and the ceiling raised to 64

Box: win-laptop (Laser2025-20), physics adapter RTX 4080 Laptop (Vulkan) — R15's reading of the RTX 5090. Model and level
(rung_record.py now): claude-opus-5-5, high.

## Measured

The repro reproduces here: `headless --scene scenes/sun_disk.json --steps 400` warned at step 4 (16 passes, 34 rounds) and
steps 340–347 (16 passes, 348–1,532 rounds; linux-pc's numbers to step 342, within ~5 % after). A dump before each of those
steps reproduces its warning exactly from `--load-dump … --steps 1`.

**The probe** (temporary, since removed): heat.wgsl's update kept the previous iterate in the free `p` plane, and headless
wrote P5's `frozen` (T⁰, C), `faces` (each D_f) and `solve` (the last iterate) planes. Those planes define the step's
whole system, so an f64 model (numpy, the twin's algorithm: Newton passes, Jacobi-PCG, the factor-2 clamp, u ≥ u_vac)
re-solved the GPU's own frozen system, at the twin's tolerances (10⁻¹² solve and heat, 10⁻¹⁰ cell) and at the GPU's
(10⁻⁵, 10⁻⁶, 10⁻³). Scripts: scratchpad `d37.py` (described here; not in the repo).

δ at 16 passes: up to 5.5 × 10⁻⁵ (step 343). At 64 passes: ≤ 1.1 × 10⁻⁶.

**Steps 340–347: a heating front, one cell a pass.** Cold cells near the floor (T⁰ 0.05–0.18, C ~10⁻⁴–10⁻³) are heated to
T 2–14 within the one step. Traced in the f64 model (step 344): cell (324,150) stays at T⁰ 0.083 for 9 passes, then
doubles six times (the ×2 clamp), then converges to 13.6. Cells farther out start at passes 12–14. A cold cell's
linearisation (c_i ∝ 1/T³ in u; in T-form, its emission ∝ T³) makes it absorb nearly all the heat it receives that pass,
so it passes on none until it is hot. This is the state, not the GPU: the twin's own tests need 20–41 passes on these
systems. Variants measured on 340 and 344 (f64): no upper clamp → 19 and 31 passes. The tangent T as the update (Newton
in T) → 17 and 35. Tangent when heating, ×4 clamp → 13 and 24. None fits 16 → the lead's question.

**Step 4: f32 drift, GPU only.** The 20 cells that fail (|ΔT| > 10⁻³ T at pass 16) are the disk's eight corner spots.
They are thin rim cells (C 2 × 10⁻⁶ – 7 × 10⁻⁵, c/ΣD 5 × 10⁻⁵ – 8 × 10⁻²: heat-free or next to heat-free), all moving the
same way by a ratio of 0.9985 a pass. There is no flip. They are ~5 % from the f64 answer, and the step's heat is converged
to 6.6 × 10⁻¹⁰. Each pass's start shift and ~2 rounds move them along their slow mode. On the Quadro, step 4 converges in
5 passes (reports/p5_cost.md), so this is adapter-dependent. At 64 passes it ends at 24.

## The ruling and the build

The lead, 2026-10-14, question tool: «Raise to 64 (Recommended)» → contract §0.5 [M0-D37]; the plan's Superseded line.
`MAX_PASSES` is 64 in heat.wgsl and in reference/heat.rs (the twin's, which `SLOTS` reads: 4,146 → 4,290 slots). The rounds
ceiling (4,096) and every tolerance are unchanged. 2 code files, 3 lines changed. No check changed, so no red-arm.

  (heat.rs:519) on the RTX 4080 Laptop, with the same numbers at 16 and 64 passes. Green on linux-pc's RTX 5090 (M0-D36). It
  is this scope's first win-laptop run since the implicit step.
- **timing red on win-laptop** (filed **M0-D39**): `a_frame_longer_than_the_query_set…` (1000 steps at `SLOTS` rounds, M0-T121)
  hangs past the scope's 900 s with 16 passes (`SLOTS` 4,146). With 64 (`SLOTS` 4,290) it loses the device at 106 s, and the 4
  cases after it fail on the lost device. It is the scope's first win-laptop run since M0-T121, and it is red either way.
- **The twin's rounds ceiling** (4,096) would stop the twin's 10⁻¹² solve on sun_disk's steps 342–347 (4,537–11,979 rounds
  in the f64 model). The twin never runs sun_disk (its worlds stop at 128 cells), so this was not raised; a twin scene with
  such a front would hit it.
- **Thin cells' T under f32** stays ~5 % off the exact answer at step 4's corners, as M0-TJ4 measured on rings (34–69 %).
  It is graded by heat.
- **P5's cost on the Quadro** (M0-T122's 200-step window) changes only at step 4 there, and step 4 already converged in 5
  passes on the Quadro. Not re-measured (R12: timing is the Quadro's, linux-pc).
