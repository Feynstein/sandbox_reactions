## Declared choices
- X is read only where both states hold Σ ≥ Σ_floor / 1e-4 = 1e-4 (100 × Σ_vac). Unmasked, 5 of the disk's 1,608 rim cells (Σ ≈ 3e-6,
  3 × Σ_vac) read |ΔX| up to 1.06e-3 in x_H (cells (54,31), (9,31): 0.7511 against 0.7500). My reading, not proven: those cells sit at the
  vacuum threshold, where whether a neighbour crossed Σ_vac this step (f32 against f64) decides whether P8's floor matter (pure H,
  1e-8 per cell) mixes in — 1e-8 / 3e-6 ≈ 3e-3. The band stays 1e-4; only the cell set is named. The contract's G-REF does not say which cells.
- The disk's Δt is bound by gravity's term, not the CFL (hence the plant moves its sim time 0.11 %); the Sod case carries the CFL separation.
- Both runs take exactly 200 steps (not the same sim time): a short Δt reads as a state difference, which is how the plant is seen.
