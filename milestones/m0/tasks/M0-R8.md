## Findings not acted on

  that heats ~1,500× in one step needs ≥ 11 passes. That is the lever on the "frozen" steps, beside M0-R9's smaller Δt. It is named in
  the report for M0-R9 and the lead, not filed: it is a contract fork ([M0-TJ4] (3)), and M0-R9 is next on the same ground.
- **The probe's stash survives `restart()`**: a hidden state that would break determinism. M0-D85's `Deliver:` carries the reset.
