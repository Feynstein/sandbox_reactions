## Declared readings (the contract is silent; cheap to reverse)
1. **u against the limits "within 1e-4" holds where the blend has converged, not across the whole table for one pair.** The blend reaches 2K₂x^{3/2} like (K₂/K₁)·ln(1+y)/√x:
   for the shipped electrons at x = 1e8 that is 4.6e-3 (neutrons: 1e-2), so the shipped tables' high ends are not within 1e-4 of the ultra-relativistic closed form. The test checks
   the table's high end with a pair whose transition lies at the bottom (K = 100/1), its low end with K = 1/100 and the shipped table's low end (x² within 1e-4 at x = 1e-8), and the
   shipped pairs' exact integral at 1e-12 and 1e20 (within 1e-6). Likewise P's limits (1e-6) are at x = 1e-12 and 1e16, outside the table (P is not tabulated).
2. **Extrapolation:** below 1e-8 u follows K₁x² (exponent 2, exact there); above 1e8 the last segment's log-slope continues.
3. **ε_th's floor**: returned as `floor_added = Σ(floor − raw)` energy per area, for the ledger's `floor_added` (§2.9); the caller books it.
4. **μ is an argument** (`thermal`, `temperature`), not stored: it is per cell (1/μ from the composition).
