- **The measures never move.** Σ r²/D = 1.104 × 10³⁸ from round 4 to 4,096 against a bound 9.10 × 10¹⁰; 1.017 × 10³⁸ of it is
  cell (32, 32) — r −1.913 × 10²⁰, D 360 (100 of it toward vacuum), c 3.2 × 10⁻¹⁵ (heat-free), u 5.31 × 10¹⁷ where its neighbours
  hold 7–840: the pass's answer takes its u down ~15 orders. The heat measure 7.30 × 10³⁵ against 1.22, from (32, 31). The true
  residual reads the same.
- **No cell moves in any round.** x, p, r, w, s of (32, 32), its neighbours and cells of tile (0, 0) are bit-identical from the first
  round to the last, while the machine counts rounds; both parities' partial sums are rewritten every slot with the same values — the
  rounds run and write back what they read, so the cells get α = 0 and β = 0.
- **The term:** γ = 1.1037 × 10³⁸ and η = 1.2753 × 10³⁸ are both above 2¹²⁶ (8.5 × 10³⁷). The GPU divides as a × (1/b) and flushes
  the subnormal 1/b to 0, so α = γ/η and β = γ/γ_prev read 0 and CG stands still — not the ε D u rounding floor the block guessed.
  The f32 sums themselves stay finite, so neither [M0-D93]'s nor [M0-D154]'s exits fire.
- **Why M0-D94's constants converge the cut:** a_r 2e-5 halves u, γ quarters to ≈ 2.8 × 10³⁷ < 2¹²⁶ — the same cut on a_r 4e-5 with
  the quotients scaled gives M0-D94's numbers exactly (17 passes, 3,518 rounds): the system is the halved one times 2.
- The first START's heat-free shift (one level over all of the cut's heat-free cells) moved (32, 32) by −1.035 × 10¹⁵ (0.2 %) and
  drove (40, 40), at T 44, to u −1.0 × 10¹⁵ — measured, part of M0-D156.
