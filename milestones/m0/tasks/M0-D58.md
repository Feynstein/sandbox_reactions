# M0-D58 — the heat-on runaway on a dense sub-resolution block

BUILD · linux-pc (`laserax-ai`), RTX 5090 (Vulkan) and the CPU f64 twin · 2026-10-19 · model=claude-opus-5-5 level=high
(`rung_record.py now`). Raw output: logs/M0-D58.log (the repros before and after), logs/M0-D58.radiation_force.log,
logs/M0-D58.radiation_force.redarm.log.

## The cause, measured

The scene: M0-R4 M9's C/O 5,000 — a 2 × 2 rect of Σ 1,250 at T_floor 0.05 at (31, 31) of a 64 × 64 bounce world, gravity,
hydro and heat on, the shipped constants (the probe's four 1 × 1 rects are the same block).

1. **Reproduced** on the tree as it stood: `headless --adapter "RTX 5090" --scene <block> --until sim_time:0.5 --dump-every 1` →
   `SR-WARN heat unconverged` at steps 9, 11, 12, `SR-HEADLESS DONE steps=17 sim_time=0.922189 until=met`, exit 0 — M9's numbers to the
   digit (E at the dense cell 2.16 × 10⁶ at step 1, NaN at 12; finite mass 0.003 at 14).
2. **Not f32, not the GPU.** A scratch probe (in the scratch copy below, never in the tree) stepped the twin with every pass the scene
   runs — `Twin` with heat on (`headless --cpu-reference` runs it with heat off, headless.rs:288, so its run is no comparison) — beside the
   GPU, step by step. The twin runs away too: dense-cell E 1.75 × 10⁹ (step 9), 1.07 × 10¹³ (11), 4.3 × 10³⁰ (15), Δt down to 5.8 × 10⁻¹⁸;
   the GPU tracks it to ~4 digits through step 9. At step 10 the GPU's P5 hits 4,096 rounds (passes 19) and commits δ = 4.82, which only
   speeds the GPU's end.
3. **P4's push, not P5.** The twin's passes by hand (P2, P4, P5, P8, P9), the radiation force on and off:
   - push off: finite, every P5 converged in 5–6 passes, the block cools (dense-cell E 2.2 × 10⁶ → 4.5 × 10⁵ by step 16), P8's
     `floor_added` ~0;
   - push on: P8's `floor_added` 6.35 × 10⁶ (step 2), 1.05 × 10⁸ (4), 7.0 × 10⁸ (7), 6.67 × 10¹⁰ (9), 3.3 × 10¹⁴ (11), 2.7 × 10³⁵ (15).
4. **Why.** After step 1's P4 the block's rim cells (Σ 114.6) are shock-heated to T ≈ 2,300, where a_r T⁴ is 7,366 × the cell's own
   heat Σε_th (10⁹–10¹⁷ × in the thin cells later). The push f = κΣF/c_sb is a radiation-pressure gradient (≈ u/3 a cell in thick gas)
   whose energy reservoir u = a_r T⁴ is not in E; §2.4.4 takes its work out of the gas's thermal energy, thousands of times smaller.
   At step 2 cell (32, 30) gains KE 9.6 × 10⁵ on E 2.5 × 10⁵: raw Σε_th −8.0 × 10⁵, floored by P8, which makes the energy real; the
   moving gas shocks, heats, radiates, and the next push is larger.

## The fix (the lead, 2026-10-19: «Cap the work (Recommended)»)

§0.5 [M0-D58], §2.4.4 amended in place: the cell gains s·Δt·f, s = min(1, s*), s* the positive root of
|p₀ + s d|² − |p₀|² = 2ΣA, A = Σ(ε_th − T_floor/μ) of the cell after P4's flux differences and gravity's source (s = 0 when A ≤ 0);
E unchanged. `Hydro::push_share` (crates/sr-physics/src/reference/hydro.rs) and `push_share` (crates/sr-engine/shaders/hydro.wgsl),
the root in its cancellation-free form on each sign of p₀·d. Where s = 1 the update is the old one bit for bit (the slab cases
unchanged). The trade-off, put to the lead: in radiation-dominated gas the capped push is weaker than nature's; the physical fix —
a_r T⁴ in E and a_r T⁴/3 in Π — was the other option, not taken.

## After the fix

- M9's runs on the fixed binary (`--until sim_time:0.5`, RTX 5090): the block 105 steps, C/O 8,000 (sinks on) 106, C/O 12,000 (one
  cell, sinks on) 171, n 8,000 (sinks on) 106 — exit 0, no `SR-WARN heat unconverged`, mass invariant drift 2.7e-8, 2.0e-8, 6.3e-8,
  4.5e-8.
- Scope `radiation_force` 7/7 GO (15 s); red-arm GO: `radforce-zero` RED 5 failed, `push-uncapped` RED 4 failed (uncapped, the GPU
  block empties to 6.1e-5 with 4,592 non-finite values; the twin block panics in P5's clamp on a NaN T).

## Findings not acted on

- **P8 still refills heat on the heat-on block**: `floor_added` 5.04 × 10⁵ over 105 steps (28 % of the start's thermal energy
  1.80 × 10⁶), against −41 over 270 steps with heat off (co5000_off). The probe places it after P4, in cells P5 cooled to near the floor
  that carry kinetic energy — the hydro update's E − KE under the floor, the cap's s = 0 there: the scheme's positivity in cold fast gas,
  bounded, booked, decaying — not the push and not a runaway. It bears on G-CONS's energy over whole lives (M0-T106).
- **The twin's P5 panics on a NaN T** (`clamp(0.5 t, 2 t)` with NaN, core f64.rs:1585) where the GPU commits NaN — seen only under the
  `push-uncapped` plant; a non-finite state is already an error (exit 5).
- **The GPU's unconverged commit**: at its 4,096-round ceiling the GPU committed δ = 4.82 (step 10, before the fix) — in contract
  ([M0-TJ4] (6) commits the last iterate with (5)'s closure) and gone with the cause; no case in the tree reaches the ceiling now.
