The lead's ruling (2026-10-16): «U_th = 0.05|W|, keep 100× (Recommended)» → contract §0.5 [M0-T62]; §2.10's preset keeps 0.1|W|.
The scene's T is 2.3666 (U_th/|W| = 0.0500000 on the grid's W; the test grades 1e-4).
