## 2. The fix (heat.wgsl `build`)
`c * u * u` is kept, so every bit any step had is unchanged. Where it is not finite it is re-formed without u²:
(C/Δt)(T/4) u, or (C/Δt) T u under constant_diffusivity. This is §0.5 [M0-D154]. The doc comment on `HeatReport::warning` now
names [M0-D93]'s exit as well.

**Tried and reverted:** re-forming the rounds' measures too (r²/D and (C/Δt) ê² as (b/√d)², (√a b)²). Step 4,443 then runs to
4,096 rounds, 22 steps warn, and the life goes non-finite at step 4,736 (energy, cell (298, 195); exit 5). Those round-0 exits
protect the state. The lead: «Keep them as exits (Recommended)».
