## What exists now
- **The scheme (contract §1.4.2), twin and shader alike.** The Hancock predictor kicks each face state by half a step of
  the cell's g — beside the common flux delta, per face: momentum += ½Δt·Σ_f g, energy += ½Δt·Σ_f (u_f + ¼Δt·g)·g, i.e.
  the face's velocity moves by ½Δt·g and its thermal energy is untouched (the energy source over the half step is the
  work at the half-kick's mid velocity). The predictor also returns the cell centre's (Σ, Σu, Σv) half a step on: the
  centre plus its own flux delta plus ½Δt·Σg on the momenta. The update adds Δt·Σ_½ g to the momenta and
  Δt·(Σu)_½·g to the energy — time-centred on the predictor's half-step velocity. A ghost's g is its edge cell's
  (used only by leave's ghost predictors; bounce's wall reads the own face alone). Without gravity nothing changes: the
  source is a branch, not an added zero.
- **Twin** (`sr_physics::reference::hydro`): `Hydro::step_with(st, dt, Some((gx, gy)))`; `step` = `step_with(.., None)`.
  `predicted` returns `([Face; 4], mid)`.
- **GPU** (`shaders/hydro.wgsl`, `step/hydro.rs`, `step/mod.rs`): g bound at binding 14 (`grav`) in `hydro_update` and
  `hydro_edges` (it recomputes the predictor, M0-T35's flag); `Edges._a` is now `gravity` (1 when P2 runs; a 2-word
  placeholder is bound otherwise), so `set_edge_mode` keeps the flag. The next state is **two buffers of 7 planes**
  (`next_lo` = Σ, Σu, Σv, E, x_H, x_He, x_C; `next_hi` = the other 7), §2.2's order, `put_next`/`get_next` — the update
  binds hydro, species_a, species_b, next_lo, next_hi, dt_rec, grav + eos's table = WebGPU's 8; 7 planes at 2048² =
  117 MB, under the 128 MiB binding (a const assert says so). `Hydro::new` takes `gravity: Option<&wgpu::Buffer>`;
  `with_passes` passes `Gravity::field` when P2 runs, so headless runs with both switches now couple them.
- **Scene** `scenes/cold_disk.json`: 128 × 128, leave, disk (64, 64) a = 40 Σc = 1 t = 0.05 H, gravity + hydro on,
