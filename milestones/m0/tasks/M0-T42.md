## What exists
- `crates/sr-engine/shaders/heat.wgsl` (new) and `crates/sr-engine/src/step/heat.rs` (new, `pub mod heat` in step/mod.rs): P5 as
  M0-T41's twin in f32. 39 dispatches a step, in order: `heat_begin` (1 invocation) · `heat_cells` (T₀ floored as Eos::thermal,
  C = Σ/μ — 0 marks vacuum —, κ, 1/μ, κΣ frozen) · `heat_stiff` (each cell's own Σ_f K_f / C) · `heat_faces` (K and F of each box
  cell's −x/−y faces, and the +x/+y faces of the box's last column and row; capped where stiff) · `heat_rate` (Σ_f K_f / C into an
  atomicMax on the f32's bits — exact, order-free; M(Y₀), the rate of loss to vacuum; side.flux = the mean of a cell's two
  faces) · `heat_plan` (1 invocation: Δt_p, s, the time covered, the record) · `heat_stage` × 32 (RKL2 stage j, returning at once
  when j > s; each told j by its own uniform) · `heat_commit` (E += C (Y_s − Y₀), booking.heat's `radiated` += R_s). All but two
  are box-sized (indirect, the box's tiles), the two are `[1, 1, 1]`: the latch reaches every one without a change.
  Scratch: frozen planes (7), faces (K then F, x then y), Y and R (3 slots each); the record (`HeatReport`: stages, need, worst,
  dt, dt_p).
- `Step::with_heat(device, state, booking, eos, physics, &passes, Option<&HeatSetup { side, test }>)` — `with_passes` is it with
  None, so no existing `Passes` literal changed; `Step::read_heat` → `HeatReport` (`warning()` = `SR-WARN heat stages <s>` once
  any step needed > 32); `Step::read_heat_flux` → the face fluxes in the twin's `Flux` layout.
- headless: the scene's `heat` switch builds P5 with its `test` flags; after each chunk it prints `SR-WARN heat stages <s>` for
  each new largest s. timing.rs's `disk` and dump.rs's `as_headless` build P5 too (they are "the step headless builds").
- tests/gpu/heat.rs: 9 GPU cases (`gpu_…`), `OnGpu` (P5 alone between P1, P8 and P9; Δt written as the Δt record's next Δt

- Plants: each of chi-scale, rkl2-s-minus-one, limiter-off gained the same bug in the shader (one hunk each).

## The cap above 32 stages — the lead's ruling
On sun_disk the flow spreads thin hot gas into the vacuum rim (Σ ≈ 2 × 10⁻⁶, T ≈ 5): measured 54 stages needed at step 1 (the twin
on a nearby state: 99), up to 3,803 within 200 steps. The GPU cannot record a step's s without reading it back, so it records 32.
Asked (question tool, 2026-10-14) — «Throttle stiff faces (Recommended)»: a cell whose own Σ_f K_f / C would alone need more than
32 stages over Δt has every face it touches capped at K ≤ 0.999 · (32² + 32 − 2)/(4Δt) · C/4 (the smaller gas side's C), so every
cell fits 32 stages over the full Δt; F and the booking are the capped ones; energy is kept. A step with no stiff cell is the
twin's scheme bit for bit in its decisions (the cap's trigger is per cell, not global). Should rounding still ask 33, `heat_plan`
runs 32 over the time they cover. The twin has no cap (M0-T48 flagged).
