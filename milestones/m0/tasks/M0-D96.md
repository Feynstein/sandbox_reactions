## The ruling
Asked (the question tool, 2026-10-26): what bounds Δt when no cell is at or above Σ_vac. The lead: «Floor gas's step (Recommended)»
→ contract §0.5 [M0-D96] + the Superseded mirror (m0_superseded.md). With an empty box, every cell of the world is in P8's floor state, so
§2.3.5's min over the world is the fastest floor gas's: hydrogen's fractions (1/μ = 2, Y_e = 1, the largest of the ten species), Δt =
C / [2 c_floor], and no gravity term. The other options were declined: keep the last Δt (a world empty from step 0 stays frozen); sinks
bound Δt (every sink run's bits move, so the hash and the calibration move too); the box keeps the sinks (reverses M0-D70's premise).
