## What exists now

- `crates/sr-engine/shaders/heat.wgsl`, section "The implicit step" (appended; RKL2's entries untouched): contract §0.5
  [M0-TJ4] (2), (4), (5) and (6)'s ceiling in f32, for scenes whose `test.constant_diffusivity` is set (u = T).
  - `heat_law` (box 8 × 8 tiles): each face's D_f = the shared `face()`'s K (χ C_f), unthrottled, and the start state's F.
  - `heat_open` (one invocation, a latch slot): the machine's record (mode INIT, the rounds' 16 × 16 tiles, Δt), the report
    (`stepped` unless pinned, Δt ≤ 0 or the box empty), RKL2's record zeroed with Δt; enables slot 0.
  - `heat_round` × `SLOTS` = 4,096 + 3 · 16 + 2 = 4,146 (workgroups 16 × 16): a mode machine. Each slot first sums the
    previous slot's partial sums — thread-strided then a shared-memory tree, the same fixed order in every workgroup —
    then does its mode: INIT (pass 1's system at T⁰: x = u, T, diagonal c + Σ D_f, right side c u − (C/Δt)(T − T⁰) +
    Σ_vac D_f u_vac; partials Σ C T⁰, Σ c u², Σ (C/Δt) T²), RESID (the passes' end test on the previous update — Σ C|ΔT| ≤
    1e-6 Σ C T⁰ and no cell over 1e-3 of its T — or finish; else r = b − A x from the iterate, the bounds (1e-5)² · sums),
    START (w = A D⁻¹r, partials γ = (r, D⁻¹r), δ = (w, D⁻¹r), e = Σ (C/Δt) ê²), SOLVE (stop when γ ≤ u_bound and e ≤
    t_bound, or the rounds reach 4,096, or η ≤ 0 → the Newton update: T = clamp(T(max(x, u_vac)), T/2, 2T) and the next
    pass's system; else one Chronopoulos–Gear round: β = γ_k/γ_{k−1}, η = δ − β² η_{k−1}, α = γ/η, p, s = A p by recurrence,
    x += α p, r −= α s, w = A D⁻¹r with each neighbour's r_{k+1} recomputed from the other parity). Vectors a neighbour reads
    (r, w, s) and the record and partials are in two parities by slot; a slot sizes the next in the other parity's args
    buffer (`Size::Chained`), so no dispatch writes the buffer it is sized by; a finished chain enables nothing.
  - `heat_close` (box tiles): F = −D_f (u_b − u_a) at the final T (T⁰ when not stepped: the law), side.flux, each gas cell's
    `radiated` Δt Σ_vac D_f (u − u_vac) into booking.heat, partials (Σ C (T − T⁰), R, Σ C T) per 8 × 8 tile.
  - `heat_sum` (one workgroup of 256, a latch slot): δ = −(gained + R)/held into the report; both args buffers zeroed.
  - `heat_apply` (box tiles): E += C (T − T⁰ + δ T) (δ's own precision kept; (1 + δ) T − T⁰ would round δ to f32's 1).
- `crates/sr-engine/src/step/heat.rs`: `Heat::new` picks the list by `test.constant_diffusivity` (implicit) else RKL2;
  scratch per path (`solve` 5 planes, `krylov` 6, `machine`, `partials`, two args buffers, two parity uniforms);
  `SLOTS`; `HeatReport` gains `passes`, `rounds`, `converged`, `delta` (RKL2: 0, 0, true, 0); `read_heat` reads the

- `crates/sr-engine/src/step/mod.rs` + `latch.rs` (outside Deliver's two files, needed): `Size::Chained(buffer, offset)` —
  recorded as an indirect dispatch, not one of the latch's buffers; the latch reaches the chain through `heat_open`, its

## Deviations and declared defaults

- The one-cell pulse case's peak is 100 over the floor background (the twin's case: 1 over 2.0, two runs differenced).
  At peak 1 on the GPU (measured first): σ² 46.82 against 48 and the heat +1.4e-3 — the contract's stop rule is relative to
  the whole field's heat scale (§0.5 [M0-TJ4] (4)), where a peak-1 pulse holds 1e-3 of the world's heat, and the floor (P8)
  rectifies the solve's sub-floor noise; a background of 2.0 differenced has the same scale problem. The law is linear, so
  100 is the same physics with the pulse the scale the tolerance reads. Added: the field against the twin (1e-4).
- "One dispatch a round where the box allows" read as always one: every round workgroup sums all of the box's 16 × 16
  partials (950 at 600 × 400; the cost grows as their square — M0-T122 measures, M0-T121 may split a round in two for large
  boxes).
- The passes' loop is the twin's, generic (damping, end test, 16 / 4,096), because the linear law's "check" is its second
  pass and the damping binds even on the linear law (the sharp pulse: 8 passes). What M0-T119 adds: u = a_r T⁴ in `u_of`,
  `t_of`, `slope`, D_f's radiative form, every scene onto the list.
- Files outside Deliver's: step/mod.rs and step/latch.rs (`Size::Chained`, 9 lines); five code files in all.
- tests/gpu/heat.rs: the stage-count/cap case and the RKL2 one-pass case retired (their scene is linear).
