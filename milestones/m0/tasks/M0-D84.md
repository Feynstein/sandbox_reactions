## Findings not acted on
- **The tree's 20 M₀ life left M0-R7's hard stretch.** On today's code the Jacobi life converges at 1,400 (the cap binds from ~1,314 in
  M0-R7's). The life's capped steps were not searched for further on.
- **Jacobi's capped round is 13 % dearer than M0-R7 measured** (28.6 against 25.4 µs). That is the M0-D83 round machine; Jacobi is now
  tests-only.
- **The V-cycle is slower than Jacobi on the easiest steps.** On 7.5–12 rounds a step, P5 is 1.04–1.20× Jacobi's (5.08 vs 4.87, 3.68 vs
  3.06 ms), within the 5.9 ms bound. The fixed ≈ 1.26 ms is the recorded slots' mostly zero-sized dispatches (M0-D83's flag). The hybrid
  was not needed.
- **One cell can differ far more than the integral.** On M0-R7's hard stretch, the worst cell (C ≥ 10⁻⁴) between one V-cycle step and
  converged Jacobi is up to 0.73 relative (0.20 at 600, ≤ 0.03 on the tree's stretches). The C-weighted integral is 5.6 × 10⁻⁵, (4)'s
  stopping test is integral, and M7 modelled 7.1 × 10⁻⁴ at that cell size. Where it sits (a thin, cold edge cell heating within the step
  is the likely place) was not probed.
- **The V-cycle life still has 3 unconverged steps by step 600** (Jacobi's has 24). Their passes and rounds were not probed (calibrate
  counts them, M0-D74).
