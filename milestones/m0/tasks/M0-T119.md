## What exists now

  - `face()` returns (D_f, F) on the start state: D_f = c_sb λ(R)/(κΣ)_f + k_cond/(a_r (T_a + T_b)(T_a² + T_b²)) (the
    conduction term only when k_cond ≠ 0, as the twin), or χ C_f under `constant_diffusivity`; F = −D_f (u_b − u_a).
    R and λ unchanged (the fourth powers relative to the hotter side).
  - `u_of` / `t_of` / `slope`: u = a_r T⁴ (computed a_r·T²·T², left to right, so T⁴ never stands alone), T = (u/a_r)^{1/4},
    dT/du = 1/(4 a_r T³); the identity under `constant_diffusivity`. M0-T118's machine (Newton passes, damping clamp,
    u ≥ u_vac, end tests, Jacobi-PCG, the commit) was already generic and is unchanged.

## The lead's ruling (question tool, 2026-10-14)

The block's Adversarial line asked the spot's solved face fluxes within 1e-4 of the largest of the twin's. Measured
1.91e-3. Cause, measured face by face: inside the spot neighbours differ by ~3e-7 of T (both GPU and twin), D_f = c_sb/(3 ·
2e-4) ≈ 3.3e5, so one f32 step of u (6.4e-6 × 1.2e-7) moves F by ~2.5e-7 ≈ 4.5e-4 of the largest face (5.6e-4) — the
largest gaps (1.06e-6) sit on faces whose twin flux is 2.5e-15. T matches to 1.9e-5 (1e-5 of the peak). Answer: «Grade by
energy (Recommended)» — Δt |F_gpu − F_twin| ≤ 1e-5 of Σ C T⁰ at every face (contract (4)'s heat scale), the law kept at 1e-4
of the largest. Declined: «Keep 1e-4, file a D», «Per-face f32 floor». A plan-line change in this block's test, no contract
change.
