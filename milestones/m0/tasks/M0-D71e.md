# M0-D71e — the hold's mask in dumps (§2.12.3 [M0-TG2])

Box: linux-pc (laserax-ai); every GPU run on laserax-ai-server's RTX 4090 (R18). model=claude-opus-5-5 level=high
(`rung_record.py now`, the heading's rung).

## Built
- `sr_engine::dump::Dump.held: Option<Vec<u32>>` — one word per cell, 1 held, 0 free. `to_bytes` writes
  `"held": true` in `resume` and the mask as one more little-endian f32 plane after every other (the 14 channels, F's 2
  and the rate's 1 when present) only when `held` is `Some`; with `None` the key is absent and the bytes are a
  pre-[M0-TG2] dump's.
- `Dump::from_bytes`: `resume.held` optional (absent or `false` reads none held); refused: the flag set with no plane
  ("header.resume.held: true, but no mask plane follows the last"), a plane under an absent or false key ("… not true,
  but a plane follows the last"), a value other than 0.0 or 1.0 ("… the mask's cell i is v, not 0 or 1"), a flag that
  is not a boolean. A body one plane short with `rate` and `held` both set is named as the mask's (the last plane);
  one plane long with F and no rate as the rate's (unchanged), else the mask's.
- `dump_state` reads `State::read_held` and keeps it only when a cell is held; `load_dump` uploads the dump's mask, or
  an all-zero one when the dump holds none (so a reused state never keeps a stale mask), and refuses a mask of another
  length than the world's cells.
- `headless::twin_dump` writes `Twin::held_mask()` the same way (`Some` only when a cell is held).

## Cases (`dump`, 12 → 14)
- `a_held_run_through_a_dump_equals_200_straight_steps_and_the_mask_dropped_visibly_does_not` — a 128 × 64 world, P2
  and P4 on the `leave` edge, no P5, shipped constants: an 8 × 8 core of Σ 20 at rest at ε_th = 1.5 η_hold ε_cold
  (inside the hysteresis band), uploaded held, in Σ 2 gas at T 1 falling onto it at 0.25. 200 straight against
  100 + dump + `load_dump` into a fresh step + 100: the dump bytes bit for bit, the mask among them. The step-100
  dump's mask read by hand as the 15th plane (no F, no rate) equal to the GPU's. Measured (RTX 4090): 64 cells held at
  step 100, all 64 inside the band (ratio 1.5000); the same dump with key and plane dropped by hand: `load_dump`
  empties the mask (the fresh state was uploaded all held to prove it), and 30,484 state values differ at step 200.
- `the_mask_follows_every_plane_flagged_held_and_a_header_without_it_reads_none_held` — by hand on a CPU-built 64 × 64
  dump: with F and the rate, the mask is the 18th plane; with neither, the 15th; round trips; none held → no key and
  the same planes as before; the five refusals above; scenes/heat_ceiling.bin (step 2,517) and heat_overflow.bin
  (step 16,127) read as before, none held, `to_bytes` their own bytes.
