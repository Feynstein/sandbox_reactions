## The ruling
The lead's answer, 2026-10-13: «1e-6 at step 200 (Recommended)». Every sum is graded at 1e-6 at step 200. Mass and
species are graded at 1e-6 at 2,000. The blob is graded on its masses.

- Cause of the sun_disk miss: gravity's FFT sizes (box 256²–512 × 256, world 2048 × 1024) differ in round-off, and the
  collapse grows that difference. It is not the floor: lowering Σ_vac leaves it at the same order.
- The box is [232, 136, 136, 128] at step 200 and [0, 0, 600, 400] at 2,000, because matter reaches the leave edges
  (escaped.mass 3.24). From then on the box is the whole world.
- Cause of the blob's momentum miss (3.4e-6): the vacuum reset in its wake. The momentum lost (1.02e-2) is about the
  vacuum_reset mass (1.01e-3) × the wake speed (~10). With Σ_vac 1e-10 it falls to 1.4e-8. §2.7 [M0-T19] says this
  momentum is booked nowhere. Not a defect.
- The blob's leading edge moves 0.625 cells per step in chunk 0 (the initial expansion), then 0.375, 0.375, 0.31, 0.31, 0.31.
- Escape asymmetry (first try's finding 2): with gravity off the escapes are equal. With gravity on, escaped.mass differs
  by 2.75e-3 while Σ + escaped + vacuum_reset agree to 6.3e-8. The mass leaves by another route in the two diverged
  trajectories. Not a defect.
