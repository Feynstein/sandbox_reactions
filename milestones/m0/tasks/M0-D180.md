# M0-D180 — the painted Sun-like star outgrows the world

Model/level: model=claude-opus-5-5 level=high (`rung_record.py now`, 2026-11-15, at the launch). Box: linux-pc (laserax-ai), RTX 5090.
**Deviation (R18):** every probe below ran on linux-pc's RTX 5090, not laserax-ai-server's RTX 4090 — the block's repro was the 5090's
(M0-T105's) and the runs were read against it; the 4090 reading of Verify 1 and the claim are owed there.

## The cause (measured, RTX 5090)
Probe: a scratch module of the GPU test binary (`run_scene_captured` with `--load-dump`, the progress hook every 200 steps on 16-step
frames; removed before the handoff — `git diff` on main.rs shows M0-T104/T105's lines only). Logs: `logs/M0-D180.*.log`.

1. **The paint lands faster than the star can answer.** touch_paint.json's 51 frames (+5,125.5 sb, 1.66 × the star) land in ≤ 7
   steps (0.08 t.u.; τ_dyn 1.03). From the mid-main-sequence dump (M 3,092.8, R 14.9, T_c 46.4, virial share 0.98) the star reads
   virial 0.51 at step 70,223 and falls in: T_c 46 → 210 by step 70,463 (v_r −7.3), L_nuc 1.8 × 10³ → 2.7 × 10⁶, the core bounces
   (v_r +12) and ≈ 1,840 sb is unbound by step 71,615 (`probe_mid_paint_fine.log`). **Reactions off** (the same dump and paint): the
   same fall to T_c 165, a soft bounce, settled at R 15 with 0 sb unbound (`probe_noreact.log`) — the unbinding is the burn at the
   overshoot, not the bounce.
2. **The painted star does not fit the world.** Its envelope reaches R 200–212 (the edge is 200 cells from the centre) and, under
   `leave`, ≈ 3,100 sb has left by +6,000 steps. Paced so no burst happens (one frame every 64 steps, 3,264 steps ≈ 20 τ_dyn, `step`
   triggers): virial ≈ 1 throughout, no overshoot — yet the 8,200 sb main-sequence star swells to R 93 → 200 (R 108 at X_H,c 0.33;
   G-END's 8,144 sb cloud: R 56 at X_H,c 0.35) and loses 5,132 sb through the edges, reaching an iron core at 3,285 sb and ending
   white_dwarf at 3,020 sb (`life_paced_64.log`). The difference from the cloud: a 3,093 sb half-burnt core under 5,125 sb of pure
   hydrogen (species `H`, X_H 1) — a lighter, more extended envelope over a μ-gradient.
3. **Bounce keeps it.** Under `--edge bounce` the same lives keep every gram: the burst paint → iron_core, supernova at step 109,344,
   black_hole (`life_burst_bounce.log`, 59,112 steps, 6 min 53 s); the paced paint → supernova at 109,203, black_hole
   (`life_paced_bounce.log`, 8 min 37 s). The envelope fills the box (R 270–290); the ejecta cannot leave and fall back.
