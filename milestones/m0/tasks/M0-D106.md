# M0-D106 — `timing`'s first case on win-laptop: a submission that never ran, and a frame cut to fit

Box: win-laptop (Laser2025-20), NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 Laptop GPU, driver 581.29 (nvidia-smi), Vulkan. Raw output:
logs/M0-D106.log (probes, loaded rounds), logs/M0-D106.timing.log, logs/M0-D106.timing.redarm.log.

## Measured

- **Reproduced idle.** `verify.py timing --task M0-D106` on the unchanged case, CPU load 3 % (verify: 1.48 → 2.6 of 22):
  NO-GO, timeout 900.20 s. With the wait bounded (below), case 1 read: encoded 229 ms, `submit` returned at 5.7 s, the wait
  timed out at 125.7 s. Through the wait the GPU was idle — P8, 210 MHz of 3105, 5 W, 0–4 % (nvidia-smi every 5 s) — and
  no nvlddmkm event was logged: the submission did not run, it was not slow. The process stayed after the case's panic
  (its CPU time flat) until the harness killed it — the case's later teardown waiting on the same submission.
- **Where the case waits.** The only blocking wait in case 1 is the test's own `frame()`:
  `device.poll(wait_indefinitely())` after the submit (the engine's `Step::poll` never blocks). `SR-ADAPTER` prints after the
  device exists, which is why the hung logs stop on that line.
- **The third sighting was a GPU reset.** System log, nvlddmkm id 153 («Error occurred on GPUID: 100», \Device\Video3) at
  2026-10-29 00:35:53: both arms of the lead's red-arm (logs/M0-V11-win.timing.redarm.log, started 00:35:02, builds 40.9 s
  and 45.7 s) were in case 1 then; every later case read «Parent device is lost». The 10-14 18:12:27 event falls in M0-D37's
  run (the 1000-step frame, M0-D39's). Neither 900 s hang (10-14 18:13–18:28, 10-28 20:25–20:40) has an event. Other
  events (10-19 18:35–18:36, 10-28 22:17 during the sweep's burn_cell arms) are not `timing`'s; not followed.
- **The frame's size.** 128 × 128 disk, 240 steps at ROUNDS 256: 11.8 ms a step (M0-D65's GO log), ≈ 2.8 s of GPU in one
  submission — the case's comment claimed «far inside» 2 s. The cost is not linear in rounds (128 × 128: 96 → 7.2 ms, 64 →
  6.3 ms a step): most of a step is the passes' fixed dispatches on this GPU (~4 µs each).
- **Slots the disks use (one step a submission, shipped constants):** 128 × 128, steps 0–239: at most 42 (step 2).
  64 × 64, steps 0–254: at most 18; its front at step 255 (65 slots), peak 357 at step 258. 96 × 96 carries at step 2 at
  ROUNDS 48; 48 × 48 is refused by `WorldConfig::new`.
- **Frame probes (240 steps, RTX 4080 Laptop):** 64 × 64 at ROUNDS 48 → 3.98 ms a step (≈ 0.96 s GPU), 1.64 s wall; at 64
  → 4.36 ms (≈ 1.05 s). No stall in 10 probe frames at ≤ 96 rounds.

## Not proven / not acted on

- Why a ≈ 2.8 s submission sometimes never starts on this driver (no reset, GPU idle) is NOT PROVEN: the measured fix is
  the frame's size, ≈ 2.9× less GPU time and ≈ 4× fewer dispatches; ≈ 0.96 s is inside 2 s by 2×, not «far».
- The 64 × 64 front is 15 steps past FRAME (128 × 128's was 27): a constant change that moves it earlier fails case 1 by
  its named carry assert, not silently.
- The engine's own `wait_indefinitely` calls (headless.rs, state.rs, latch.rs, step/mod.rs) are untouched — not this
  case's wait; headless and the game don't submit frames this large.
