## Declared readings (cheap to reverse)
- «clamped to the span» is read as sliding the 0.1 window inside the span, never cutting it: a literal per-end clamp of a one-cell world at the
  temperature floor gives [1.0, 1.054], under the contract's own H − L ≥ 0.1. Cases: `a_one_cell_world…` (T_floor → [1, 1.1]), `matter_past_the_span…`
  ([9.9, 10], [3.9, 4]), `the_range_from_bins…`.
- A cell past the span counts in its end bin (the contract says «over the span»; dropping matter would leave a hot star's H short).
- World sizes are multiples of 8 (§2.8, ≥ 64): there is no partial workgroup; the last-row/column case uses a 72 × 64 world and the corner and
  the two far ends.
