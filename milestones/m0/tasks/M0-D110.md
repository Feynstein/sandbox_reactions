## The lead's ruling (2026-10-29, at M0-D110)
«Gas-speed term only (Recommended)», shown as: on a step whose P0 paints or drops, P0 recomputes the CFL (gas-speed) term over the
re-fit box and takes the min with the Δt from step n−1, which keeps that step's gravity term (P2 hasn't run on the edited state yet);
steps with no paint or drop stay bit-identical. The other option was the whole reduction, gravity included, which reads g where the
box just grew — values P2 left from an older box, or zero. Written as contract §0.5 [M0-D110], its mirror in m0_superseded.md.

## Measured on the way, not acted on
- **A step continued by P5's carry** (source read only — NOT PROVEN): under the latch, a submission whose first step continues a
  carried step zeroes that step's P0 (`latch_hold`), so the GPU's queue applies at the next step, while `deposits_at` follows the CPU's
  count (`Pending::at_step`), which marked the first. That next step paints with no re-reduction, on P9's Δt — the fault this task
  fixes, for one step. `Step::run` never starts on a carried step (`run_gated` ends only when every step has), so headless and the
  tests cannot reach it; the app's frames can (a frame may end on a carry). Not fixed here: closing it needs the GPU to mark the step
  that applied the queue (edit_gate) and `dt_refresh` to read it, recorded on every step that may apply — a second thread. Flagged to
  M0-T89 (the brush, the first caller of `queue_edit` from frames).
- The energy invariant on the edit's step still moves by ≈ ΔW (empty world −1.6590 with ΔW −1.6427; cold box reported in the log) —
  M0-D109's, unchanged.
