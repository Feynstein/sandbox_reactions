## What changed
- `crates/sr-engine/shaders/floors.wgsl` — `non_finite(v)` (exponent bits, reduce.wgsl's test, no float comparison) and
  `fractions_non_finite(cell)` (any of the ten planes). Both P8 kernels, `floor_cells` and `renormalise_species`, return
  at once for such a cell: its ten fractions stay as read, Σ, u and E untouched, nothing booked. `renormalised` and the
  floors' rules for every other cell are unchanged. The header comment says so.
- `crates/sr-engine/tests/gpu/floors.rs` — `run_p8` split into `run_p8` + `run_p8_on(c, world, planes)`; new case
  `a_cell_with_a_non_finite_fraction_is_left_for_the_guard`: the 64 × 64 field with NaN x_C in a vacuum, a cold and a
  hot cell, +Inf x_Fe in a dense cold cell (all-≤ 0 fractions), −Inf x_n in a cold cell → one step → each planted cell
  bit-identical in all 14 channels (its fraction still non-finite), both booking slots 0; the other 4,091 cells
  bit-identical to the clean field's run.
- `crates/sr-engine/tests/gpu/dt.rs` — new case `the_guard_names_a_nan_fraction_that_p8_would_renormalise_away`: NaN x_C
  at (20, 10) before step 0 (M0-T20's probe) → one step → `NonFinite { step: 0, x: 20, y: 10, channel: "x_C" }`, the
  cell's ten fractions bit-identical to the planted ones.
- `tools/pb/verify.json` — `floors` expected 6 → 7, `dt` 5 → 6, each desc names the M0-D18 case (the block's Pass counts).

## Decision taken (cheap to reverse), declared
- Deliver says "its fractions written back as read, not renormalised, the floors kept as they are". My reading: the
  cell is left wholly as read — `floor_cells` skips it too. Applying the floors with a NaN composition would leave a
  non-vacuum cell's E alone (the comparison is false) but give a vacuum cell E = NaN and book NaN `floor_added`, and the
  guard (hydro channels first) would then name `energy`, not the fraction that caused it. The floors' rules for finite
  cells are untouched. No contract text changes: §1.3.2 P9 / §3.3 exit 5 already require the guard to see the value.
