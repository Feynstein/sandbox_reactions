## What exists

- How a life is read: summary.json holds the ledger's start and end only, so each life runs under `Progress::checkpoint_every` and reads each checkpoint's observers' snapshot (`ledger.last`, f64 as strings) and dump (Σm|u|). Invariants are recomputed from the terms (every energy term printed, signed).

## Readings the contract leaves open (declared; mine)
- mass: at each checkpoint |inv − start| / start. momentum: |p − p₀| over the running peak of Σm|u| (grid + sinks) — the instant Σm|u| is printed, not graded (a quiet main sequence has a tiny one beside its collapse and would grade a correct integrator red on f32 noise). energy: per window between two records, |W| the smaller of the two, τ_dyn the largest the readout showed in the window.
