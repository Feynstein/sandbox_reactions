## Declared readings (cheap to reverse)
- A cell is one unit of area, so q is a power per cell and per area; Σq·Δt is the energy booked `neutrino_deposited`.
- ΣS_ν = 0: no FFT, q = 0. ΣD = 0 (every cell is neutron star): q = 0, `deposited` 0 — the energy is not handed anywhere.
- D clamped at 0 after the multiply: FFT round-off can leave −1e-17 where the exact sum is ≥ 0; ΣD is taken after the clamp, so
  the normalisation stays exact.
- Vacuum cells are not excluded (the contract says nothing); their Σ = 1e-8 makes their share negligible.
- The plan's "f_dep applied twice" read as the share counted twice (2·f_dep of the emitted), the reading that "no-cap" names.
- The twin's step driver (`Twin`) does not run P7 yet; the contract's order would put it after P6, with S_ν from `react::Pass::s_nu`.
