# M0-D218 — the web frame loop held at two frames in flight; the first frames' cost named

Box: linux-pc (laserax-ai), Chrome 157.0.8103.78 headless, WebGPU on the Quadro RTX 4000. Model: claude-opus-5-5, level high
(`rung_record.py now`, 2026-11-25, the heading's rung).

## (1) The first frames' 0.7–2.2 s, measured and named
- Not GPU execution. Per-pass WebGPU timestamps (tasks/M0-D218.passes.mjs, logs/M0-D218.passes.log): one step's 18–19 passes
  take 35–61 ms of GPU time, the P5 heat/multigrid pass 21–29 ms of it; the first frame on `/` is 56.5 ms.
- Not the pipelines. The warm-up's 99 sync creations and the real build's 99 are both done in the GPU process 0.26–0.70 s
  after each burst (tasks/M0-D218.gpuproc.mjs, logs/M0-D218.gpuproc.log).
- It's Chrome's GPU process handling commands on the CPU. A Chrome trace (tasks/M0-D218.dawn.mjs, logs/M0-D218.dawn.log) puts
  1.87 s of 4–10 s in `WebGPUDecoderImpl::HandleDawnCommands` (one flush up to 642 ms) and 27.7 ms in `Queue::Submit`.
- What it handles: a census of the WebGPU calls (tasks/M0-D218.census.mjs, logs/M0-D218.census.log). A one-step frame records
  3,937, 2,956, 2,236, 1,696, 1,576, then 1,216 `dispatchWorkgroupsIndirect` calls, each with its setPipeline and setBindGroup,
  and its submit-to-done time falls with them: 1.04, 0.90, 0.66, 0.57, 0.52, 0.41 s. That's ≈ 0.25 ms of GPU-process CPU per
  indirect dispatch on this box.
- Named: P5 records `Heat::rounds()` slots a step, and those start at the SLOTS ceiling. Only a readback teaches it `most`
  (`Heat::learn`), and then it falls by at most a quarter a read. The latch zeroes the unused dispatches, so the GPU barely
  runs them, but Chrome still decodes and validates every one. With no backpressure no readback came back, every queued frame
  was recorded at the ceiling, and a tick's frame cost the GPU process ≈ 1 s. That's the runaway: ≈ 70 frames, then
  mapAsync blocked 70 s.

## (2) The fix — crates/sr-app/src/desktop.rs only
- `Sim::frame` holds at `FRAMES_IN_FLIGHT = 2`: a pass that finds two frames with steps unread plans, owes and submits nothing,
  not even the render. The next frame's wall time spans the wait. That's §1.8.3's own bound («Δt_est the last Δt read back,
  ≤ 2 frames late»), enforced, not changed. So at most two `latch.readback` buffers exist (Step::encode_frame reuses a free
  slot). `Sim::frame` returns whether it ran, and a capture's `frames` count only frames that ran, so a held pass doesn't
  shorten a scripted step.
- The presented-frame screenshot is one request at a time. It's asked again only once it's known lost: a frame sent after the
  asking pass has read back with no reply, or nothing is in flight and 1 s has passed (`SCREENSHOT_RETRY_S`).
- Both targets get it, desktop included. The desktop's swapchain already paced it, so a hold there is rare. desktop, capture
  and measure_ui are GO.
- After the fix (logs/M0-D218.after.log, M0-D216's trace with no rAF delay): "ready" at 4.3 s on `/` and on the preset (was
  48 s idle). Three maps before "ready" (two `latch.readback`, one `egui_screen_capture_buffer`), never more than two latch
  maps pending.
