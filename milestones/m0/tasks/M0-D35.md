1. **The runaway pass, not D34's step-44 state.** Under the Gamow law the step-44 state (E 1.666365e4, X_H 0.742629) no
   longer runs away in one step (T 140 → 160; GPU and twin ΔE 8.9e-5 apart, an ordinary step, where D34 read P6 at ≤ 5e-5
   in f32). The case's point is the runaway, so the twin marches the field's cell (Σ 30, T 100, H | He) at the block's Δt
   until the pass that reaches iron (47) and the GPU runs that pass from the twin's state.
2. **"Each group within 10⁻⁵ relative" read against the pass's nuclear energy** (Σ_g |booked_g|, the twin's), not each group's
   own value: the groups that carry the energy agree to ≤ 4e-8 of themselves, but the trace groups C and Ne (C_burn and
   Ne_burn on carbon and neon burnt as fast as C_alpha makes them; 1.6e-6 and 3.6e-7 of the pass) read 1.29e-5 / 1.28e-5 of
   their own value — f32 cancellation in ω + ∇ω·ΔX for a species in quasi-equilibrium, the same on every run, not the
   scale. The clip plant still reads 4.7× over the threshold. To grade against each group's own value instead, the
   threshold for groups under ~10⁻³ of the pass would have to be f32's, a lead's call.
3. **Two extra cells** (M0-D34's NaN cell and He_burn's peak) guard the scale's edges; their energy comparisons floor at what
   f32 resolves of the cell's E (2^-23 E), since their ΔE sits under it.
