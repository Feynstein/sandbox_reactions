# M0-T121 — P5's hard steps carry over into the next submission (speed gives first)

Box: linux-pc (laserax-ai), physics adapter RTX 5090 (Vulkan). Model and level (rung_record.py now): claude-opus-5-5, high.

## What exists now

Contract §0.5 [M0-TJ4] (6), built:

- **The rounds a submission records** (step/heat.rs): each step records the first `Heat::rounds()` of P5's 4,146
  `heat_round` slots, always even, in [8, 4,146]. The caller can fix it (`Step::set_heat_rounds(Some(n))`); otherwise
  the engine chooses. It starts at 128 (past the 84 slots of the Sun disk's hardest early step). After each read-back
  it moves to the most slots any step of the submission used (`Report.most` = 2 + 3 passes + rounds, a GPU running max
  that each submission clears first), plus a quarter, plus 4. It rises at once and falls by at most a quarter per read.
  "Rounds" in (6) is read as slots: recorded `heat_round` dispatches.
- **The gate** (latch.wgsl, step/latch.rs). A step with P5 always records under the latch's sizes, frames and bare runs
  alike, with three extra dispatches:
  - `latch_hold` before P0: when a step is `held`, it closes P0–P5's opening (`zero_sizes`, the box saved) and re-arms
    slot 0 of parity 0.
  - `heat_park` after the recorded slots (heat.wgsl, bare, idempotent): when the chain is still `live`, it zeroes the
    words those slots wrote and sets `rec[1].slot` to 0.
  - `latch_carry`: when the chain is live, the step is carried — `stopped` 2, `held` 1, every later size of the
    submission zeroed as a fired latch zeroes them. When the chain has ended after a hold, it restores the box and opens
    the sizes for the commit and P6–P9 at the step's own index (`dt.steps − 1`).

  `latch_end` restores the box. `latch_check` counts the step in the submission it ends in, so `steps_run` and
  `advanced` count a carried step as any step's (§1.8.3).
- **Runs**: `Step::run(n)` on a step with P5 submits under the latch with the slow-down off, waits for each submission
  and reads the record (steps ended, `held`) until n steps have ended. A submission after a carry holds the carried step
  alone. `Step::run_counted` returns the number of submissions. `encode` is now private: it has no caller outside
  `run`.
- **Reports**: `FrameReport.carried`. `latch_fired` now means `stopped == 1` only. `StepCost.ended`. `step_ms` and
  `pass_ms` are now per step ended: the timed recordings' mean, scaled by recordings over ended steps. So a carried
  step's rounds in every submission are its own, and headless's `P5 heat` sums them.
- **Dumps**: `resume_records` refuses (asserts) while a step is held: a dump comes only at a step's end.
- **headless**: prints `SR-WARN heat unconverged step <n> passes <k> rounds <r>` for every step that reached the
  ceiling. The source is heat.wgsl's log of 256 entries, cleared at each read (`Step::heat_warnings`); the 256-step
  chunk can never outrun it. `--timing` weights each batch by the steps it ended.

## Tests

- heat_stiff `mod stiff`, G-STIFF (d)'s carried case on both scenes. One-submission step (every slot recorded, 1
  submission), then the same step from the same start at 8 slots a submission: bit for bit (state, radiated, F at every
  face, side.flux, record) in exactly ⌈slots/8⌉ submissions — heat_hot 41 for 321 slots, heat_rim 41 for 326.
- New plant `carry-commit-early` (tests/plants/, registered in heat_stiff): `latch_carry` never carries → RED, exactly
  the 2 carried cases fail.
- dump: `sun_disk_200_steps_carried_over_submissions_of_8_rounds_dump_the_engines_own_choice_byte_for_byte`. The engine's
  own choice took 1 submission for 200 steps. At 8 slots a submission, bare took 201 submissions and frames of up to 50
  took 122 frames, 119 of them ending held. All three dumps are byte for byte. The scope's expected count went from 8 to
  9 (verify.json).
- timing: `a_frame_longer_than_the_query_set…` fixes the rounds at `SLOTS` (the lead, 2026-10-14: «Fix rounds in that
  case (Recommended)»). The 128×128 disk's step 330 needs ~1,500 slots, so the 1000-step frame carried there otherwise.

## Deviations (declared)

- **Files**: 9 code files against the ≤6 ceiling — the shaders heat.wgsl and latch.wgsl (Deliver named no shader, but
  the carry is GPU logic), timing.rs (the lead's answer), plus verify.json and one plant patch. About 430 new lines.
- **heat.machine**: now also COPY_DST.
- **latch_carry's bindings**: it hit the 8-storage-buffer limit, so the zeroing of the slot words moved to heat.wgsl's
  `heat_park`.
- **Bare runs**: a bare run of a step with P5 now runs `latch_check` (slow-down off: it never fires). It takes P6's flag
  and moves `latch_since`, so a headless dump's `resume.latch_since` is now the frames' value. Headless runs agree with
  each other byte for byte (dump scope GO).

## Measured, not acted on

- **sun_disk, headless, RTX 5090, 1000 steps** (a temporary probe, since removed): P5's Newton passes hit the 16-pass
  ceiling at step 4 (16 passes, 34 rounds) and at steps 340–347 (16 passes, 348–1,450 rounds). Step 4 does so in a
  submission with no carry, so this predates M0-T121 → filed as **M0-D37**.
- Slots per step on sun_disk: ≤ 84 over steps 0–255, up to ~1,500 at steps 340–347. The engine's choice rose 128 → 1,880
  through that stretch, then fell back (1,410, 1,058). Each hard step took 2 submissions (one carry).
- The cost of recording more slots than used (zeroed chained dispatches) is M0-T122's to measure on the Quadro. The
  constants `FIRST_ROUNDS` (128), the quarter margin and the quarter decay are the knobs.
