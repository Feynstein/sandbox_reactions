## What exists now
- `crates/sr-engine/src/step/mod.rs` — `Timing {Off, Auto, Wall}`, `CostSource {GpuTimestamp, Wall}` (`name()`:
  `gpu-timestamp` / `wall`), `StepCost {source, steps, step_ms, pass_ms: [Option<f64>; 10], frame_ms}`.
  `Step::set_timing(device, queue, timing) -> Option<CostSource>` (off by default, so every other scope records exactly
  what it did before); `Step::read_cost(device, queue) -> Option<StepCost>` for bare steps (blocking; tests, headless).
  A private `Timer`: one 4096-query timestamp set + resolve buffer; every compute pass of a step (and a frame's
  `frame begin` / `frame end`) gets `timestamp_writes` at its boundaries. A batch = a frame, or the bare steps between two
  `read_cost` calls; it times its first whole steps while 12 passes' queries + frame end's 2 still fit (≈ 509 steps at
  today's 4 passes/step), the rest run untimed.
- `step_ms` = mean over timed steps of (first pass's beginning → last pass's end), so inter-pass gaps count;
  `pass_ms[p]` = mean ms per timed step in pass p (step 0's initial reduction is booked to P9); `frame_ms` = frame begin +
  end. Wall fallback: wall time of the batch ÷ steps, no pass named.
- `crates/sr-engine/src/step/latch.rs` — `FrameReport.cost: Option<StepCost>`; a frame is one batch: its resolved
  timestamps ride in the same map-on-submit readback (records 128 B, then up to 32 KiB of ticks); `poll()` still never
  waits.
- `crates/sr-engine/src/gpu.rs` — the device requests `adapter.features() & TIMESTAMP_QUERY` (WebGPU's
  `timestamp-query`, a web and native feature; never `_INSIDE_PASSES` / `_INSIDE_ENCODERS`).
- `crates/sr-engine/src/headless.rs` — `--timing` (a bare flag) → `summary.json` `timing`: `{"per_pass_ms": {"P0 edits",
  "P1 dt", "P8 floors", "P9 reductions"}, "step_ms_mean", "source"}`; batches (256-step chunks) weighted by steps timed.
- `crates/sr-engine/tests/gpu/timing.rs` — 5 cases (see testing.md's `timing` row); `tests/gpu/main.rs` — `mod timing`,
  and the adapter case's « features empty » now reads « features == adapter's & TIMESTAMP_QUERY ».
- Scope `timing` in tools/pb/verify.json (5 cases, physics adapter); plant tests/plants/timing-skip-pass.patch (P8 left
  out of per_pass_ms by the summary).

## Deviations / defaults taken (cheap to reverse)
- summary.json's `timing` carries a third key, `source` (the block's « the source named »), beside §2.12.2's
  `per_pass_ms` and `step_ms_mean` — additive; a PLAN task can write it into §2.12.2 or drop it.
- The headless case runs `sr_engine::headless::run` in-process (the function sr-app's binary forwards to), not the built
  binary: no second build in the GPU scope.
- `timing` is a mechanism scope on the physics adapter, not a ⏱ scope: no speed is graded (G-FPS / G-TOP stay the
  Quadro's). Costs printed are for the log.
- On wasm32 the wall fallback has no clock without a new dependency (`web-time` / `js-sys` not in sr-engine): a browser
  without `timestamp-query` gets `cost: None`. Flagged to M0-T24.
- The adapter scope's check changed (features assertion) → red-armed in this task: GO, `adapter-case` RED.
