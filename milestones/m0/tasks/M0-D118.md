# M0-D118 — web[ready]'s first frame on Chrome 156's D3D12 backend

Box: win-laptop (Laser2025-20, RTX 4080 Laptop, Chrome 156.0.8078.48), 2026-10-29. Rung: model=claude-opus-5-5 level=high
(`rung_record.py now`). Raw output: logs/M0-D118.log (the probes, the readings), logs/M0-D118*.web.log (the scope runs).

## Measured — where the 17–32 s went
A scratch probe (`pipes.mjs`, playwright-core from web/node_modules, the smoke's Chrome flags; not kept) wrapped
`GPUDevice.prototype` in the page to record every shader module and pipeline the app creates, then replayed each compute
pipeline alone in a fresh browser with `createComputePipelineAsync`:
- The app creates 15 modules and 75 pipelines (73 compute, all `layout: 'auto'`) within 1.3 s of load; the GPU process then
  compiles each sync `createComputePipeline` in turn — the app's first submit completed at 16.2 s, ready at 16.6 s.
- Sequential replay, the tree as found: 18.5 s for the 73; `hydro.wgsl:hydro_edges` 9.6 s and `hydro_update` 2.9 s of it,
  `react_cells` 0.7, everything else ≤ 0.4 s each.
- Cause in hydro.wgsl: DXC inlines every call. `hydro_edges` carried five copies of the Hancock predictor (`cell_faces`,
  four faces each evolved: one for its own cell, one per `switch` arm of `boundary` for the ghost) and four `hll`s;
  `hydro_update` twenty `hll`s (four `edge_flux` calls of four each, plus four); `face_state` held `evolved` twice and
  `read_at` held `read_cell` (with its EOS) twice. Ablations (hydro_edges, warm): one predictor ≈ 0.85 s, the EOS in
  `read_cell` ≈ 0.4 s, `evolved` ≈ 0.3 s, `hll` small; making `mc`/`slope` branchless changed nothing.
- Which of the two moved it (the shaders since 2026-10-20 or Chrome 156) was not measured apart: the five-predictor
  `hydro_edges` is the seat either way.

## What changed
1. `crates/sr-engine/shaders/hydro.wgsl` — the same arithmetic, fewer inlined copies:
   - `boundary` predicts only the two faces it uses (`cell_face`: one Hancock step, one `evolved`), its own and the ghost's
     facing one (`side ^ 1`), in a loop whose count is read from `edges` — DXC unrolls a constant count, and two inlined
     predictors cost ~1.7 s where the loop's one costs ~0.9 s. `hydro_edges` calls `boundary(x, y, side, half)`.
   - `edge_flux` takes the HLL flux instead of computing it: every edge branch passed `hll` the same (lower, upper) pair as
     the inner face, so `hydro_update` runs one `hll` per face and clamps it at the world's edge.
   - `face_state` runs one `evolved` (the kick folded into the delta first); `hancock_face` picks a face before evolving it
     (the ring's old if-chain, now shared); `read_at` runs one `read_cell` for both edges; `cell_faces` is gone.
   - Sequential replay after: 9.0–12.0 s for the 73 (the box's load moves it ±30 %); hydro_edges ~1 s, hydro_update ~1.2 s.
     ready: 11.2–12.2 s on a quiet box, 16.0 s inside `verify.py web` (right after trunk's build) — still over 15 s.
   - A loop over `hydro_update`'s two Hancock steps (own, ring) saved 0.14 s — not kept.
2. `crates/sr-app/src/web.rs` + `desktop.rs` — the parallel warm-up (the lead, 2026-10-29: «Dry-run warm (Recommended)»):
   - Measured first in the probe: the 73 compiled async in parallel take 4.0–5.4 s; the same descriptors created sync
     afterwards take 0.1 s (Chrome caches by content — also when the async pipelines were dropped).
   - `warm` (web.rs): in eframe's creator, a validation error scope is pushed, `createComputePipeline` is shadowed on the
     raw `GPUDevice` (`wgpu::Device::as_webgpu`) by a closure that hands the descriptor to `createComputePipelineAsync` and
     returns an invalid pipeline (entry point `sr_warm_no_entry`: nothing compiles twice), `SandboxApp::dry_run` builds the
     app's device work once and drops it, the property is deleted and the scope popped; `Promise.allSettled` of the async
     creations resolves when all are compiled.
   - `Warming` (web.rs) is the eframe app meanwhile: it clears to `bg.space`, then becomes the `SandboxApp` built after the
     warm-up — `srState` "ready" is still that app's first presented frame (desktop.rs's screenshot flag). A build error is
     reported as before (`error.no_adapter` with the detail).
   - desktop.rs: `Sim::new`'s device work moved into `Sim::parts` (no change to what it builds), shared by `Sim::new` and the
     wasm-only `Sim::dry_run` / `SandboxApp::dry_run`.
   - The console: the page's two old lines only (a favicon 404, Chrome's powerPreference note) — no WebGPU error escapes
     the scope. 146 sync compute creations recorded: 73 invalid (the dry run), 73 real.
