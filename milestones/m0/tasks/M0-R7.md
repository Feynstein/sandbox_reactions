## Runs (laserax-ai-server, RTX 4090, idle; logs/M0-R7.log, logs/M0-R7.bench.log)
- **The life to step 1,600.** It ran twice; the second run kept the dumps, with the same ceiling counts as the first (deterministic).
  `heat_unconverged` 25 by step 800, then from step 1,314 one every step: 299 by step 1,600.
- **The hard and easy stretches, 20 steps each.** From the s_1400 dump, every step stops at 4 passes (once 5) and 4,096 rounds,
  δ 5.6–8.3 × 10⁻³, ~114 ms wall. From the s_600 dump, 89–357 rounds and 4–7 passes. From the s_1300 dump, 1,093–1,318 rounds,
  converged until 1,314.
- **`--timing`, median of 3.** s_1400: step 106.536 ms, P5 104.571 ms. s_600: 6.165 ms, P5 4.703 ms. heat_rim: 6.900 ms, P5 6.759 ms.
  heat_hot: 2.521 ms, P5 2.433 ms. sun_disk: exit 6, `preset_mass_sb: missing`.
- **The microbenchmark,** 3 runs: a dependent dispatch costs 3.3 µs (≤ 75 × 50) to 6.1 µs (600 × 400); a V(1,1) round 264 µs; V(2,2)
  327 µs; Chebyshev-8 66 µs; the fused-round replica 12 µs at 600 × 400.
- **The model** reproduces the GPU's rounds where comparable: step 1,301 1,155 vs 1,156; heat_rim 486 vs 472; heat_hot 402 vs 331. The
  report's M6–M7 come from it.
