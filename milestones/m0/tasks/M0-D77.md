## Choices (declared)
- Saves land on frame boundaries (frames end at 1 + 16k on a fresh life: saves at 49, 97 for M = 48) — "every M steps" means the first boundary at or
  past the multiple, as the progress tick does. The alternative, `--dump-every`'s chunk shortening, would have moved frames and so events.
- The periodic pair only: the final dump of a cut life is not a checkpoint (it is removed with `dumps/`); a stop loses at most one interval.
- Adapter guard is `--adapter`'s query against the snapshot's name; with no `--adapter` any adapter is accepted. physics_hash is the assets' (a test's
  `overrides` are not hashed).
- headless `--continue` itself still checks nothing of adapter or physics_hash — only calibrate's guard reads them.
- "Ends at once" exists in headless (a restored `until` that already holds); a periodic checkpoint cannot hold one by construction, so it is a guard.
