# M0-T28 — the CPU twin's FFT and convolution (detail)

- API (`sr_physics::reference::fft`): `FftPlan::new(n)` (any power of two ≥ 1; twiddles each from its own sin/cos) ·
  `plan.transform(&mut [Complex], inverse)` (forward unnormalised, inverse conjugate / N) · `fft2(data, plan_x, plan_y,
  inverse)` (row-major `y * nx + x`) · `padded_size(len)` (smallest power of two ≥ 2 × len, 32…2048, else
  `FftError::BoxOutOfRange`) · `Convolver::new(kernel)` / `.convolve(field, w, h)` / `.cached_sizes()`.
- Kernel: `kernel(dx, dy)` with dx = x_target − x_source, sampled at signed offsets over the padded grid
  (index i < n/2 → i, else i − n; the entry at n/2 is never reached). Not assumed symmetric. K(0, 0) is the caller's
  (§1.4.2's −3.525494 G for gravity).
- Convolver caches plans per length and the kernel's transform per (px, py); a 50 × 41 box reuses 48 × 40's 128 × 128.
- Tests (10, `bash tests/cpu.sh sr-physics reference`): DFT 1e-12 on 1…128, delta/wave, inverse identity, 2D DFT,
  Parseval ×2, convolution vs direct sum 48 × 40 (1e-10, rim cell by cell), corners (no wrap), single source,
  cache count, padded-size rule and refusals.
- Plant: tests/plants/fft-twiddle-sign.patch flips the twiddle sign → 3 tests red (DFT, delta/wave, 2D DFT).
