## Declared defaults (cheap to reverse; the contract is silent)
1. **Cold pressure off is a legal state.** M0-T33b/T36 switch cold pressure off through `overrides` (K's = 0). So each K pair is on (both > 0) or off
   (both 0); half a pair is refused naming the zero key. Σ_N ≥ 2 (K₂ₑ/K₁ₑ)² is checked only while the electron pair is on; the m_tov/m_ch bound only
   while both pairs are on.
2. **m_tov/m_ch = 8 (K₂ₙ/K₂ₑ)²** — §2.7's own figures (M_ch = 1.77 K₂ₑ² Y_e³ with Y_e = 0.5; M_tov = 1.77 K₂ₙ²), bound [1.4, 2.0] inclusive, error key `k2n`.
3. **m < ν of every late record** (§2.5.3) is checked: `m_nu` must be below each of the six `nu_*` keys.
4. **Sanity classes where the table says "—" or only "> 0":** every key is finite; the scale constants are > 0; the switches that may be off
   (K's, `kappa0`, `k_cond`, `a_nu`, `f_dep`, `kappa_dust`, `t_dust`) are ≥ 0; `block` is a whole number ≥ 1. Nothing is looser than the contract.
5. **Stand-ins:** `Standins::SHIPPED` (both off) is what `shipped()` uses; `f_dep` cap is 0.1 while S2 is off, 1.0 when on; `kappa_dust` must be 0 while S1 is off.
   Only a PLAN amendment changes the shipped switch (§1.6.4).
6. **elements.json is pinned to the ten species** (the state's channels are ten): the file must hold exactly §1.5's keys, A, Z, paintable in order;
   names, symbols and colours are checked for shape (non-empty, `#RRGGBB`) and, in the test, against the table verbatim.
7. **Not checked here:** `c_sb > 2 × calibration's max_gas_speed` (§2.7) needs calibration.json (G-CAL, later); Q_H's τ_nuc ≥ 10 τ_KH is measured (G-SQUEEZE).
