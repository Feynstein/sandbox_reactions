## Method
Each probe books −(its candidate's per-cell momentum) into `held`'s two slots (`booking.held`), which no cell uses before the
hold starts (step ≈ 20,500 here). Booking never feeds the dynamics: every probe life is the base's bit for bit (23,772 steps,
t 193.273634), so a probe's `held` less the base's is the candidate's sum over the life. `hydro_update` already binds 8 storage
buffers (WebGPU's default), so the probes that book from it raise the device's limit to 16 (probe binaries only).
