# M0-D83 — P5's multigrid preconditioner, part 2: the V-cycle in the round machine

Ran on model=claude-opus-5-5 level=high (`rung_record.py now`, the heading's rung). GPU iteration on laserax-ai-server (RTX 4090,
`SR_TEST_ADAPTER="RTX 4090"`, the tree rsynced over; no git push), the claim run on linux-pc (laserax-ai).

## What exists now
- **heat.wgsl.** `Parity` gains `mg` (its second word). On the V-cycle a slot is a group: its `heat_round`, then `mg_level0`
  (at the iterate, `start` 0), `mg_coarsen` a level, the V-cycle (`mg_smooth`/`mg_restrict` down, `mg_coarse`, `mg_smooth` up) and
  the new `heat_matvec` (w = A z face by face, the slot's four sums: zᵀr, zᵀw, the heat measure, rᵀD⁻¹r). Each is
  `Size::Chained` on the multigrid's 39 entries (`MGA_*`, three words each) after the slots' words of the args buffer its
  `heat_round` writes (`args_next`): `mg_size` sizes them over the box's levels — the levels after a RESID that goes on, the V-cycle
  and the matvec after a START or a round, nothing otherwise. START and a round write their r into level 0's right side; a round
  takes p = z + βp where Jacobi takes D⁻¹r; (4)'s u-measure stays (r, D⁻¹r) (the matvec's fourth sum), so the stopping test is
  unchanged. `heat_park` zeroes both parities' multigrid words at a carry; `heat_sum` already zeroed whole buffers.
- **Why no stale group runs.** A slot that does not run (the step ended, or the latch zeroed it) finds its group's words zero: every
  running slot on the V-cycle writes all 39 entries, and the two slots around a step's end (an UPDATE or INIT, then the ending RESID)
  write none on — one in each parity's buffer. A later step of a carried submission reads the words `heat_park` zeroed.
- **step/heat.rs.** `Heat::slots` — Jacobi's first `rounds()` `heat_round`s as before, or `rounds()` groups (`mg_rounds[j]`, then
  parity j % 2's group). `rounds()`, `learn`, `most` and `SLOTS` count slots, a group one slot: the learnt count is in V-cycle
  rounds, never dispatches. `set_default_precond` (tests only: the preconditioner every P5 built from then on starts with — it reaches
  `headless` in-process; the GPU tests run one at a time), `Step::heat_precond`. Pipelines built once each (the round's and the
  multigrid's), bind groups per parity. **step/mod.rs** records `h.slots(rounds)` between P5's head and `heat_park`.
- **Tests.** `on_both_preconditioners!` (tests/gpu/heat.rs, used by radforce.rs) turns each GPU case into `<case>::jacobi` and
  `<case>::multigrid`; `on_precond` sets the default and puts Jacobi back on drop. 14 GPU cases × 2: heat_gauss 14, heat_implicit 8,
  heat_limiter 8, heat_stiff 8, radiation_force 11 (verify.json `expected`). Plant `tests/plants/vcycle-carry-restart.patch`.

Every carried run bit for bit the one-submission step (state, radiated, F, side.flux, record), on both preconditioners.

**The carry falls mid-solve on the V-cycle — shown by the plant.** `vcycle-carry-restart` zeroes z in a continuation's first slot
when that slot is a round (`rec.slot == 0` is only ever a continuation there): it reds exactly heat_stiff's two `::multigrid` cases
(records 45 vs 44 rounds on heat_hot, 12 passes / 114 rounds vs 5 / 55 on heat_rim), Jacobi's two green — so on both scenes a
submission of 8 slots ends between two V-cycle rounds of one pass. The pigeonhole check (rounds ≥ 8 × passes) would not prove it on
heat_hot (44 < 56), so the plant is the proof; no assertion was added.

**The fork asked: heat_gauss's edge leak on the V-cycle.** Its GPU pulse holds the edges' radiated sum to 1e-7 of the pulse (M0-D38's
ruling, f32 round-off). The V-cycle read 5.9e-7. Probed per pass (logs/M0-D83.probe_leak.log, the server copy only): until pass 14
both preconditioners keep the edge cells at T_floor + 7.5e-10 and δ ~1e-9; from pass 15 the V-cycle's solves meet (4)'s measures in
2 rounds with δ ≈ 4e-7 (positive, falling to 1.9e-7 by pass 24), and (5)'s closure lifts every cell by δ T ≈ 2e-8 a pass — the
floor's edge cells radiate it. Within (4)'s 1e-5; σ², the heat and T against the twin all hold (T 2.7e-5 of the peak vs Jacobi's
5.1e-5). The lead, 2026-10-22: «V-cycle at 1e-5 (Recommended)» — contract §0.5 [M0-D83], the plan's Superseded, the test's band.
