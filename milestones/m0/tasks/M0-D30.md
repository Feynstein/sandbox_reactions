## What changed — crates/sr-engine/shaders/hydro.wgsl (the scheme unchanged)
- `hydro_update`: one workgroup per 8 × 8 tile (the same `Size::Indirect` at `ARGS_TILES`; hydro.rs's dispatch sizes
  unchanged). A vote first (`atomicOr`, then `workgroupUniformLoad`): a tile with no moving cell copies its in-box cells
  and stops. Else: 132 of the 12 × 12 primitives read once with `read_at` (ghosts included) into workgroup memory; the
  ring's 32 predictors (invocations 0–31, the face chosen before `evolved`, so one `evolved` each) and the 64 own; then
  the x faces exchanged through the same memory, x fluxes, then the y faces, y fluxes. Every barrier is reached by all 64
  invocations (no early return before the last one but the uniform one). Workgroup memory: `tile` 2,304 f32 (9,216 B,
  primitives 144 × 14 component-major, then faces 128 × 18) + two u32 — inside §6.2.2's 16,384 B; storage buffers
  unchanged (8 in the update).
- The predictor split into `hancock` (slopes, face states, flux delta, mid, g), `face_state` (evolve, kick under g),
  `predict` (all four); `cell_faces` = `predict(hancock(read_at …))`, used by `hydro_edges` only. `boundary` split: the
  ghost's face then `edge_flux(side, own, ghost)`, which the update calls with the face from its tile.
- Docs: hydro.rs's module comment, physics.md § The GPU's layout, testing.md's `sod` row.
