## Declared defaults (cheap to reverse)
1. **escaped is booked per boundary face** (`EdgeFaces`, 2(w + h) slots), not per cell: matter leaves only through
   the world edge (§2.8), each boundary face is computed by one invocation, and 14 world-size planes would cost
   235 MB at 2048² and blow P4's storage-slot budget (3 state + φ + F + 1 escaped = 6 of 8 now).
2. **One frame's worth per slot**: passes add into their slots through a frame; the frame's reader (summary, ledger)
   reads, then calls `clear`. The ledger keeps the cumulative totals in f64 (§2.9 "cumulative since the ledger
   (re)started") — the GPU slots never hold a run's total, so f32 holds only one frame's sum per slot.
3. **P5–P7 add directly into the accumulator slots** (their own cell, no atomic); §1.3.2's P9 "the per-cell frame
   accumulators … fed by P5–P7" needs no dispatch of its own.
4. **swallowed is energy only** (§2.9 lists it under energy); the mass and momentum a sink takes are the sink's
   state, which the ledger reads as `sinks`.

## Findings measured and not acted on
- **floor_added has one writer, P8.** §2.3.3 floors ε_th at T_floor/μ wherever it is computed; if P4 hydro writes a
  floored E back (rather than using the floored ε_th only for Π), P4 becomes a second writer of `energy.floor_added`
  and needs its own slot — a new term in the layout, not a shared buffer. Flagged to M0-T57.
- **§2.9 says "from fixed-order block partials"**; `book_totals` sums full per-slot planes. At 600 × 400 one
  readback is 23 cell planes + 14 edge planes ≈ 22 MB — fine for tests and headless, too much each frame at 60 fps.
  A GPU partial reduction (per row or per 4 × 4 block, fixed order) writing the same term order lets the ledger feed
  `book_totals`-style f64 sums from partials; that is the summary's / ledger's block. Flagged to M0-T57.
