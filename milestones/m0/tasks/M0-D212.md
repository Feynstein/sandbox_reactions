## The drift by term (the repro; each probe books into a term the window leaves at 0 — `held`, the tools' energy terms)
Method: M0-D199's — every sum that must be exact in three-word fixed point (`settle_fixed`, 2⁻⁸ / 2⁻²⁸ / 2⁻⁴⁸), one workgroup.

and gravity terms, the core moving in −y). M0-D194's K take-back covered only the kick's part of it.

## The fix (declared, §9 — the lead's word owed; contract §0.5 [M0-D212], §1.4.2 and G-CONS amended in place, Superseded mirrored)
When the step runs P2, the densest free cell takes back **T, P4's whole update on the momentum invariant** instead of K: the free
box cells' Σⁿ⁺¹u − Σⁿu as the next state holds it, plus the held cells' spared (booked `held`) and the boundary faces' momentum (the
f32 products booked `escaped`, two new words a face in `push_net`, written by `hydro_edges`), less the sinks' pull (the update's
product on the whole field less the one on the gas's own — a real exchange P3 books); then less the push N its shares give back,
with [M0-D199]'s rest, so the commit's whole take-back is T. In exact arithmetic T = K + N. Without P2 nothing changes (bit for bit:
`sod`'s identical rows untouched). The twin (f64) keeps taking back K, which T is in exact arithmetic.
- Cost. First version: the sum inside `hydro_push_sum`'s one workgroup (640,000 cells, 256 invocations) — 10,000 → 20,000 took
  227 s against the base's 105 s (2.2 ×). Shipped: a new dispatch `hydro_net` over the box's 8 × 8 tiles (after `hydro_edges`) sums
  each tile exactly into 8 i32 (`hydro.net_tiles`; T's three words and the tile's densest free cell), `hydro_push_sum` adds the
  tiles — the densest-cell search moves out of the one workgroup with it. The repro: 37.7 s against the base's 41.3 s.
