# M0-D82 — P5's multigrid preconditioner, part 1: the level hierarchy and a symmetric V-cycle on the GPU

Ran on model=claude-opus-5-5 level=high (`rung_record.py now`, the heading's rung). GPU iteration on laserax-ai-server (RTX 4090,
`SR_TEST_ADAPTER="RTX 4090"`, the tree rsynced over; no git push), the claim run on linux-pc (laserax-ai).

## What exists now
- **heat.wgsl's last section** (§0.5 [M0-D82]): `mg_level0` (level 0 over the box: a gas cell's own term c_i + Σ_vac D_f, −1 off the
  gas; D_f between two gas cells, 0 elsewhere; linearised at T⁰ when `mg_step.start`, else at the iterate's T), `mg_coarsen` (Galerkin
  2 × 2 aggregation, one dispatch a level), `mg_smooth` (16 × 16 tile + halo in 324 f32 of workgroup memory, 4 red-black pairs, halo
  frozen at the dispatch's start; down red first from z = 0, up black first on z + P z_coarse), `mg_restrict` (the residual summed into
  the coarse b), `mg_coarse` (one 256-invocation workgroup, every level of ≤ 256 cells, whole-level sweeps with `storageBarrier`, the
  coarsest by 50 (R, B, B, R) pairs). z down and z up are separate planes, so no tile reads a halo another workgroup writes.
- **step/heat.rs**: `HeatPrecond { Jacobi (default), Multigrid }`, `Step::set_heat_precond` (tests only), `Step::heat_first_system`
  (builds the levels at T⁰ from what a finished step leaves — frozen planes, faces, Δt — and returns level 0 with each gas cell's
  vacuum D_f and the level counts), `Step::apply_heat_precond` (M⁻¹ r over the box, the chosen preconditioner: the V-cycle on the GPU,
  Jacobi from level 0's diagonal). Two buffers (`heat.mg_coef`, `heat.mg_vec`, each ≤ 67 MB at 2048², asserted under 128 MiB), a
  12-level layout uniform, a uniform per (level, up). Every multigrid dispatch is sized for the world's level (Fixed), the shader leaving
  levels past the box's idle; the build's level 0 rides the box's 8 × 8 tiles. Not in the step's dispatch list: the round machine is
  M0-D83's.
- **tests/gpu/heat.rs `mod multigrid`** (scope `heat_multigrid`, 4 cases), `tests/plants/vcycle-no-coarse.patch`, the verify.json scope,
  the testing.md row, contract §0.5 [M0-D82], the plan's Superseded mirror.

**Symmetry, the fork asked.** The block's |⟨Mx, y⟩ − ⟨x, My⟩| ≤ 1e-4 |⟨Mx, y⟩| failed on heat_hot at 3.0e-4 on one pair whose
⟨Mx, y⟩ is 1.5e-5 of ‖Mx‖ ‖y‖ — f32's 5e-9 of the norms over a vanishing denominator. Measured by hand in a scratch copy
(`/tmp/d82/sym` on the server): the correct V-cycle ≤ 4.8e-9 normwise on every pair of both scenes; with the up sweeps red first (not
the adjoint) 4.4e-6 – 4.8e-4 normwise, every pair. The lead, 2026-10-22: «Normwise ≤ 1e-7 (Recommended)».

**The plant's margin is thin.** On 64 × 64 the tile smoother alone (4 tiles a side) is strong: without the coarse correction heat_hot
still converges in 25 ≤ 28 and heat_rim in 36 > 34 — one case red. The coarse correction's worth shows at size (below).

## Limits found
- Workgroup memory: the device is built with `wgpu::Limits::default()` (gpu.rs), WebGPU's 16 KB, so a tile over it fails pipeline
  creation on the 4090 too; `mg_smooth` uses 1,296 bytes.
- `mg_level0`'s `start` is 1 in every `mg_step` uniform today (the tests read the first pass after a step); M0-D83 builds at the
  iterate (start 0) after each Newton update.
- Sizing: 723 new lines (shader 331, engine 196, test 156, scope 29, plant 11) against the block's ≈ 600 — one artifact kind, no
  second thread; declared, not split.
