**The twin's passes** (D159_TWIN, logs/M0-D159.twin.log):
- Passes 1–15 move the whole star (13.9 % of Σ C T⁰ in pass 1).
- From pass ~15 only 1,300–2,300 cells are past (3)'s per-cell test, and the worst cell walks outward one cell a pass:
  (289, 185) → (288, 183) → (288, 181) → (290, 176) → (290, 174) → (292, 172) → (296, 171).

**The front cells** (the cut):
- They lie 18–30 cells from the core. Only 4 cells are held, at (299–300, 199–200), so the hold is not the front.
- They are pure iron, X_Fe 1.000, with Σ 0.28–0.46.
- Their E is almost all kinetic: 62–107 against E − ½|m|²/Σ of 0.3–0.9, so ε_th is at the floor and T⁰ = T_floor.
- That is the post-supernova iron shell inside hot gas, heated about a cell a pass: [M0-D37]'s front.
