# M0-T81 — the glow (laserax-ai, RTX 5090; model=claude-sonnet-5-5 level=high)

## What exists
- `shaders/glow.wgsl` (new): four compute entries — `glow_bright` (half resolution, mean of a 2 × 2 block, each cell kept when
  luminance 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B of the encoded values > 0.8), `glow_blur_h`/`glow_blur_v` (9 taps, σ = 3 texels,
  weights exp(−k²/2σ²) normalised over k = −4…4, zero outside), `glow_composite` (bilinear upsample, clamped at the edges,
  added at 0.6 to the cell image, clamped to 1). Half-resolution textures are RGBA16F.
- `render.rs`: `Renderer::render(view, …)` — Glow draws the cell image into an internal texture, then the four passes into the
  caller's target; Heat/Element/Density draw the cell image straight into the target (never touch the glow). New
  `Renderer::render_cells(view, …)` = the cell image alone (what `views` grades). No new requirement on the caller's target.
- `tests/gpu/glow.rs` (scope `glow`, 5 cases) against an f64 model of the passes from the read-back cell image; plant
  `tests/plants/glow-threshold.patch` (threshold 0.8 → 0.0): red, 1 failed.

## Deviations (keep or rule)
1. `tests/gpu/views.rs`, a T80 check, edited in one line: `render` → `render_cells`. Forced by §3.1 (`render` carries the glow
   for Glow); `views` stays 11/0/0.
2. Luminance and the add are taken on the sRGB-encoded 8-bit values (the contract fixes neither space), the 2 × 2 reduction is a
   mean, the blur is zero outside, the upsample clamps.
3. verify.json: scope `glow` added; the `views` desc names `render_cells`. testing.md: `glow` row, `views` row's pointer.

## Measured, not acted on
- **Blue-hot matter does not glow.** The luminance of the Glow view's colour for hydrogen at Σ = 0.2: 3,000 K 0.525 · 5,000 K
  0.919 · 8,000 K 0.909 · 12,000 K 0.818 · 20,000 K 0.759 · ≥ 40,000 K 0.720 (the table's end colour [158, 184, 255]). A
  luminance threshold of 0.8 therefore blooms matter between ~3,500 K and ~13,000 K and nothing hotter — a supernova's
  100,000 K+ shell would not flash through this pass (B12). The contract says "luminance > 0.8"; not changed. A max-channel or
  a linear-RGB threshold would reach it. The lead's call (a contract change, §1.11).
- A lone bright cell's halo peaks under one level of the 8-bit image (the 2 × 2 mean × the Gaussian's 0.0235 centre weight ×
  0.6 ≈ 0.9 levels): the lone-cell case proves the model and the reach; the 16 × 16 block (halo ≥ 20 levels) is the
  discriminating case.
- Not proven: a star's glow (synthetic worlds); the bloom at the app's 600 × 400 and its cost (no timing taken).
