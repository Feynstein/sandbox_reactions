## The budget — what it is for
- Each case runs its own command, so `timeout_s` is read per case (my reading of the harness's per-case
  `-- stages[<case>] · exit=… · <s>` lines — NOT PROVEN until Verify 1's whole run).
- 21,600 s = the Sun-like's step cap at the loop's measured pace, rounded to the siblings' value (touch, age,
  conservation, endings, squeeze, fit): `endings` green inside the 4090's `--all` (2026-11-23) ran 506,520 steps
  in 21,462 s = 42 ms a step → 320,000 × 42 ms = 13,600 s. So an unmet life ends red on its own cap (exit 2) before
  the harness cuts it, alone and under the loop's average pace.
- Margins: alone on the RTX 5090, ×7.7 over the Sun-like's ≈ 2,800 s; under the loop's average, ×3 over its
  ≈ 7,200 s. Not covered: the pace of the first 5,400 s of the 4090's two `--all` runs, where every ⏱ scope started
  at once (34,709 steps in 5,400 s, 156 ms a step) held for a whole Sun-like life (≈ 26,600 s). Before this
  block the Sun-like life only started after the Giant and the Massive; now the three start together.
- Adversarial: three lives at once on one GPU — the scope's cases run in parallel (jobs 8); the Giant and the
  Massive overlap the Sun-like's first ≈ 7 minutes only.
