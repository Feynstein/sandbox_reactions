## The shorter repro
`headless --scene preset:sun --steps 45000 --edge bounce --dump-every 1000 --adapter "RTX 5090"` (≈ 7 min alone on the 5090) —
the `conservation` case's life, bit for bit (M0-D194's 15,000-step window reads the same: 1.04e-6 at 15,000). As found:
|Δp| 1.24e-3 at 15,000, 9.4e-2 at 45,000; graded **7.88e-5** of the running peak (1,197, step 1,000) at 45,000.

What the life does: until step ~21,000 every cell of the 600 × 400 world holds Σ ≥ Σ_vac (no reset, the box is the world, the
walls book); then the thin gas drops under Σ_vac (221,011 cells at 22,000), the box shrinks to 120 × 128 about the star, the walls
stop booking — and from there the grid drifts (−6.3e-2, +6.5e-2) by 45,000, unbooked, accelerating with the main sequence.

## Method
Probes booking a candidate into a term the window leaves unused — `held` (0 over the whole life) and, after step 22,000, `escaped`
(the walls book nothing once the box is off them) — so the dynamics are the base's bit for bit (checked on the dumps). Sums that
must be exact (fluxes that should cancel) are fixed point, three i32 words (2⁻⁸, 2⁻¹⁸, 2⁻²⁸), added with atomics; the probes
start from the base's s_22000 dump with `--load-dump` (resume bit-identical to the straight life, checked at 23,000 and 30,000).
The ledger's grid momentum matches an f64 sum of each dump's planes to ≤ 1.2e-6 (5 checkpoints).

## The fix (the lead, 2026-11-19: «Into escaped (Recommended)», «Keep D184, settle rest (Recommended)»)
- (a) P8's `floor_cells` books the momentum a reset cell held into two new booked planes, `vacuum_reset.mom_x/_y` (state.rs TERMS
  42–43, group `booking.vacuum_reset_mom`, appended), which the ledger sums into §2.9's `momentum.escaped`; summary.json's keys
  unchanged; a pre-[M0-D199] snapshot (42 booked terms) restores them 0.
- (b) `hydro_push_sum` forms each free cell's give-back as `hydro_commit` will, t = |s·d|·N/S off the next state's momentum m, and
  sums what f32 keeps, fl(m − t) − m, exactly — each side in three-word fixed point (2⁻⁸, 2⁻²⁸, 2⁻⁴⁸, `settle_fixed`; the third
  word reduced in its own pass through `kick_part`, workgroup memory being at WebGPU's 16 KiB) —; the rest, −N − Σ kept, comes
  out of K's words, so the densest free cell gives it back with K (a P5 step without P2 finds that cell by Σⁿ⁺¹; K is then 0).
  Each cell's share stays [M0-D184]'s. The twin (f64) drops nothing, unchanged.
- Two versions failed first. (1) Two-word fixed point (proto_rest's, 2⁻²⁸): `radiation_force`'s GPU case read 4.57e-4 off its
  oracle — the slab's momenta sit under the quantum, so those cells' kept parts rounded to 0 and the whole give-back came back at
  the densest cell. (2) An f32 sum of what f32 drops, r = (fl(m − t) − m) + t (exact by Sterbenz on paper): **the driver folds
  it to 0** — the 45,000-step life came out the base's bit for bit (grid (−6.294e-2, +6.486e-2), 4.870e-6 = vac_all's) and
  `radiation_force` went green only because the settle did nothing. Integer conversions of each side are not foldable: the
  three-word version is live (the state differs from the base's after 1,000 steps) and reads 7.9e-9 / 4.4e-8 (TOL 1e-6).
