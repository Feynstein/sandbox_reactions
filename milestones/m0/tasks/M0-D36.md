## The cause, measured to the mode

The thin ring of heat_rim (Σ 2 × 10⁻⁶) is coupled cell to cell by D_f = c_sb/(3 κΣ) (λ = 1/3 frozen where two ring
cells start equal), ~10⁵ times its coupling to the disk and to the vacuum (flux-limited). Its common level, the ring's
u as a whole, is then the system's slowest mode: Jacobi-PCG's polynomial is 1 at 0, so the rounds barely move it, and
neither of (4)'s measures sees it (both weigh heat; the ring's r on that mode sits at its border, r²/D tiny). Probe:
`SOLVE_TOL` 10⁻⁶ instead of 10⁻⁵ (scratch edit, reverted) → (d) passes (L1 4.2 × 10⁻⁵) but 2,531 rounds instead of 495
and the ring's emission still −0.32 % off: conditioning, not a stop-rule threshold.

## The fix (crates/sr-engine/shaders/heat.wgsl, +64 lines, one file)

Each pass's CG starts from the iterate shifted by one Galerkin step along z, the indicator of the pass's heat-free cells
(`free_at`: c_i ≤ 10⁻³ Σ_f D_f, c_i from C_i and the pass's T): x += z zᵀr/zᵀA z, r −= (zᵀr/zᵀA z) A z. The RESID slot
computes r as before plus (A z)_i (into p's plane, which the first round overwrites) and its share of zᵀr and zᵀA z, face
by face over the faces leaving the heat-free set — so the huge ring-to-ring D_f cancel exactly (a first prototype that
summed r in f32 and took c_i as the diagonal less Σ D_f stalled Newton at 16 passes); START applies the shift. No new
dispatch, no new buffer, no Rust change, the machine record's size unchanged. A Galerkin step never increases the error's
A-norm, so a scene without heat-free cells (zᵀA z = 0: no shift) or with several regions (one shared scalar) loses nothing.

Reading declared: §0.5 [M0-TJ4] (4) fixes the method (Jacobi-PCG, f32, fixed-order reductions) and the two stop
measures, not each pass's starting guess; (3)'s « the first iterate is the pass's start state » is Newton's, kept. So
the fix changes no rule of (4)–(5) and was not taken to the lead (Deliver: a changed rule asks first). The twin is
untouched (f64 to 10⁻¹² needs no deflation; G-REF compares the same discretisation).
