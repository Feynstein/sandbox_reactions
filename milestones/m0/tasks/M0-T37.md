# M0-T37 — the twin's step driver and `--cpu-reference`

Ran on Sonnet 5.5, high (rung_record: model=claude-sonnet-5-5 level=high), linux-pc (laserax-ai), RTX 5090 for the one GPU-step build.

## What exists
- `sr_physics::reference::Twin` (reference/mod.rs): the passes built so far in §1.3.2's order on §2.2's f64 planes —
  P1 Δt, P2 gravity (when `Switches.gravity`), P4 hydro (`Switches.hydro`), P8 floors, P9 Δt_{n+1}. `Twin::pass_list` /
  `passes_run` give the GPU's labels (`Pass::label`); `first_non_finite` is P9's guard (cell, then channel).
  Worlds over `MAX_WORLD` = 128 on an axis are `TwinError::TooLarge`.
- The GPU's order: Δt_0 after P2 on the initial state; step n takes Δt_n from the end of step n−1; P9's gravity term reads
  g from step n's own P2 (the pre-hydro state's), not a fresh field of the state P9 reduces — M0-T36's flag to M0-T38,
  met here. The fresh-g loop of collapse.rs differs by 4e-5 in Σ after 12 steps of cold_disk.json (the test prints it).
- `reference::floors` (new): P8 in f64 per contract §2.7 [M0-T19] — `Floors::cell`, `Floors::apply`, `renormalised`,
  `Booked` (vacuum_reset, floor_added). Σ_vac and Σ_floor are f32-rounded (the GPU's); a non-finite fraction leaves the
  cell as read. tests/gpu/floors.rs's inline p8_twin is now a wrapper over it (its `f32_of` went unused and was removed).
- `headless --cpu-reference` (headless.rs): twin from the same scene planes, `SR-ADAPTER name=cpu-reference backend=cpu
  driver=sr_physics::reference`, the same summary.json (adapter `cpu-reference`) and dumps (`twin_dump`; the box record is
  the whole world's, `BoxMode::WholeWorld`'s form). World over 128 on an axis: exit 4, no summary. `--adapter`, `--timing`
  and `--load-dump` are refused with it (exit 4) — declared defaults: `--load-dump` cannot resume the twin bit for bit
  (a dump holds no g, and Δt_{n+1} needs step n's), `--timing` and `--adapter` mean the GPU.
- USAGE line (sr-app main.rs) and docs/agent/running.md brought to the tree's flags (M0-T27's flag).
- Scope `twin` (tests/gpu/twin.rs, 6 cases, ≈ 4.4 s warm; `bash tests/gpu.sh twin physics`), plant
  tests/plants/twin-skip-floors.patch (drops P8 from the driver: 3 of 6 cases red).
