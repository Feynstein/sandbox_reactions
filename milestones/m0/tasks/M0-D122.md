# M0-D122 — a Clear's frames in flight never reach the new observers

Ran on linux-pc (laserax-ai); model=claude-opus-5-5 level=high (rung_record.py now, at launch). 2026-10-31.

## What exists now
- `crates/sr-app/src/ui/banner.rs`: `Frames`, the routing factored out of `Sim`. It owns the in-flight queue, each entry
  `(world, forced)`, the `Watch` and the `Feed`. `submitted(forced)` tags a frame with the current world; `clear()` starts
  the next world; `read_back(time, frame)` always gives the report to `TimeControl::on_report` (Δt, sim time and owed
  time stay the engine's), then drops a frame whose world is not the current one: no latch cut, no `Watch::update`.
  The new world's `Watch` and `Feed` are built at its first frame read back, the `Watch` starting from the sim time of the
  old world's last frame. Before, `Sim::clear` built them at the click from the sim time of the last frame read back, so
  the new observers' first sim_dt also covered the old frames still in flight.
- `crates/sr-app/src/desktop.rs` (`mod sim`): `watch`, `events` and `in_flight` replaced by `frames: Frames`; `clear`
  calls `frames.clear()` once the engine has queued the `Clear` edit; `poll_frames` keeps `sim_time` and `dt_est` and
  hands each frame to `frames.read_back`; `frame` calls `frames.submitted(forced)` where it pushed `in_flight` before.
- Two tests in `ui::banner::tests`, built with no GPU on a synthetic one-star `BlockSummary` (L_H = L, §1.9.4):
  `a_main_sequence_frame_of_the_current_world_ignites` is the control: the same frame fires ignition (×1 plus banner)
  for observers new to it. `a_frame_submitted_before_a_clear_books_nothing_after_it`: two frames in flight at a Clear,
  the second one latch-cut, are read back with the clock left at ×30 and no banner; then come an empty new-world frame
  and a new-world star, whose own ignition does slow the clock.
- Plant `tests/plants/banner-clear-in-flight.patch`: every frame read back is taken for the current world's.
- `tools/pb/verify.json` `ui_banner`: expected 13→15, the plant added, paths `crates/sr-engine/**` and
  `crates/sr-app/src/desktop.rs` added (the test drives sr-engine's `Watch`; desktop.rs wires the router), desc extended.
