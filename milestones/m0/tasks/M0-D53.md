## Declared where the ruling is silent (cheap to reverse)
- **Distance** is between block centres in block units, Euclidean (integer squared distance, exact). **Ties** go to the
  heavier object, then the lower id (the selected star's default, §1.9.6, is the heaviest).
- **A wisp's blocks** are outside every listed object, so their light goes to the nearest listed object (a wisp is no star).
- **Only edge blocks are searched** (a member block with an 8-neighbour outside the box or not of its object): an inner
  block always has a neighbour of its own object nearer to any outside block, so the result is the full search's.
- **No listed object** in the box: its light is credited to none. L_ν, L_g and L_nuc stay per own blocks (not ruled).
