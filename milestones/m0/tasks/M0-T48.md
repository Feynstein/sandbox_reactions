## The lower-Σ scene: not reachable (logs/M0-T48-r2.sweep.log — 23 variants, the burn case alone)

The flags (M0-T48's first try, M0-D34, M0-D35) asked for a field burning "a few %" of its H. Measured: the field is bistable
within 200 steps — H falls ≤ ~1 % or the field runs away to Fe (100 %); nothing in between.
- Uniform, T 100: Σ 1 → 0.06 %, 3 → 0.27 %, 4 → 0.44 %, 5 → 0.97 % (E L1 4.96e-4, closest to the band of any run), 6, 7, 8,
  10 → 100 %. Uniform hotter and thinner (Σ 1 at T 150/200/300, Σ 0.3 at T 300/500) → 100 %.
- Root cause (reading): uniform matter, bounce edges, neutrinos off — nothing cools the gas, so ~1 % of H burnt heats it into
  the Gamow law's steep part; a cooling channel needs a temperature gradient (P5's flux).
- Hot or dense core (16² or 32²) in Σ 3–5 gas: P5 carries the core's heat out and the burn stays under 1 % (0.28–0.71 %), or
  the core runs away and its front ignites the whole field (Σ 4 bg + core Σ 30 T 120 → 100 %); a core in cold thin gas
  (bg Σ 0.5–1, T 20–50) still burns the whole field to Fe (100 %, E L1 1.9e-4 – 3.6e-4, sim time up to +0.25 %).
- Every variant held G-REF's bands (largest E L1 4.96e-4); none passed the adversarial without running away.
Kept: the shipped uniform Σ 30 (E L1 6.8e-5, the widest margin measured). Its X comparison is trivial (both sides end as Fe);
E and the sim time compare the full burning trajectory. The partly burnt cases above are not graded by any scope.
