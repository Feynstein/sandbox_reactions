## The test scene
A 16 × 16 iron block at Σ 400 in a 64 × 64 bounce world, gravity on (it binds the block: `M_unb` < 0.1 M, so the supernova row does not
match), hydro, heat, neutrinos and sinks off: Y_e Σ = 186 over Σ_N 160, so N_Fe converts it from the first step until the gate closes.
Measured on headless (laserax-ai, RTX 5090, then laserax-ai-server, RTX 4090): core_collapse shown from step 1, `cloud` from 97 (frames of
16); Σ 350 collapses longer (263 s for 96 steps on the 4090 against 62 s at Σ 400); Σ 800 and 1,600 end as neutron_star from step 65 and
run slower (211 s, 270 s). Without gravity every cell is unbound and the supernova row shows from step 17. Each collapse step costs
0.6–3 s on the 4090 with heat off; `--timing` over 20 steps (idle 4090): P6 3,047 ms a step, every other pass under 0.02 ms —
the collapse's time is N_Fe's in P6 (filed as M0-R10). An RTX 3060 run sat ~10 min at 0 % GPU with no output; I stopped it, NOT measured.
logs/M0-D80.probe.log.
