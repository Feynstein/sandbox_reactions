# M0-T128 — the live legend under the world

Ran on linux-pc (laserax-ai), model=claude-sonnet-5-5 level=high. Logs: logs/M0-T128.log, .ui_legend.log, .redarm.log, .changed.log.

- `crates/sr-app/src/ui/legend.rs`: `legend()` builds title, bar, ticks and marks; `ticks()` places a tick at every power of ten
  inside the range by log T (both ends when fewer than two), `spaced()` drops a label overlapping the last kept one (`label_left`
  keeps each box inside the bar); `Bars` caches the Glow bar per S; `layout()` is the centre area's one placement of world, strip
  and hint (it replaced desktop.rs's `world_rect`). The Glow bar is the render's own CPU form (blackbody table, dimming table,
  haze at Σ = 1); the test grades it against the direct formula (exact dimming, blackbody clamped to 1,000–40,000 K as the table is)
  within 2 levels at S = 3,000 K, 10^6 K, 10^9 K.
- desktop.rs `draw_legend`: title and marks' labels on the top row, 12 px bar, 3 px ticks, labels under; mark = 10 px triangle + 2 px line
  (S in `accent`, cursor in `text.primary`); the cursor's label flips left at the strip's right edge or over S's label.
- Declared: a cursor value past the shown range stands at the bar's end; tests/smoke.sh geometry updated (BELOW) for §4.2's new layout.
- Owed: M0-T129's zoom must go through `legend::layout` (the world's scale z · s) so the strip keeps its place.
