# M0-T43 — The radiation force in the flow

Box: `linux-pc` (laserax-ai; physics on the RTX 5090, R12). Model: claude-opus-5-5, level high (`rung_record.py now`:
`model=claude-opus-5-5 level=high plugin=installed`, matching the heading). Scope spelling is R17's: `radiation_force` (the

## What exists
- **The twin** (`crates/sr-physics/src/reference/hydro.rs`): `Hydro::step_forced(st, dt, gravity, radiation)` — `radiation`
  is the previous step's F as two cell-centred planes (`heat::Flux::cells`); `step_with` is it with `None`, so every existing
  caller is unchanged. In the update, after the flux differences and gravity's source, every gas cell (Σ ≥ Σ_vac) gains
  Δt·f of momentum, f = κΣF/c_sb from the cell as the step found it, κ = κ₀(1 + X_H) plus κ_dust·Z_met where T < T_dust
  (heat.rs's `kappa`, §2.4.2). E is left alone. `Hydro` now holds κ₀, c_sb, κ_dust and T_dust.
- **The GPU** (`shaders/hydro.wgsl`, `step/hydro.rs`): `hydro_update` adds the same Δt·f (`radiation_force(cell)`) when
  `edges.radiation` is 1. F is bound at binding 6 straight from `side.flux`, the buffer P5 writes after P4, so P4 reads
  the previous step's F. `Edges` grew to 8 words (leave, gravity, radiation, pad, κ₀, c_sb, κ_dust, T_dust). `Hydro::new`
  takes `physics` and `flux: Option<&Buffer>` (a placeholder when there is no P5). `set_edge_mode` keeps the other words.
  `Step::with_heat` passes `HeatSetup.side.flux()` when it builds P5.
- **The storage slot**: the update already bound WebGPU's 8 storage buffers. The Δt record is now bound to P4 as
  `var<uniform> dt_rec: vec4<f32>`; `step/dt.rs` adds `UNIFORM` to the record's usage. This follows the `abox` precedent
  (a buffer written by one dispatch and read as a uniform by another). The update binds 8 again: state ×3, next ×2, g, F,
  eos table. `hydro_edges` binds no F and still uses 7 storage buffers.
- **Tests** (`tests/gpu/radforce.rs`, `mod radforce` in main.rs): 3 cases, listed under § Measured. Scope
  `radiation_force` in verify.json (`bash tests/gpu.sh radforce physics`, expected 3). Plant `tests/plants/radforce-zero.patch`
  zeroes F in the twin's call and f in the shader (one hunk each).

## Design: why the predictor is not kicked
D30's flag suggested putting the force into hancock's kick as well as the update's source. It went into the update only:
- **Order**: F already lags a step (§1.3.2, the contract's choice), so the force is first order in time either way.
- **Cost**: a kick would read F and X_H in all 96 predictors of each tile, and P4 is already over TW's budget on the
  Quadro (M0-D30).
- **Testability**: one step with and without the force, from the same state, then differs by exactly Δt·f in each cell
  and in nothing else. That makes the oracle exact and the "E unchanged" check bit for bit.

Gravity keeps its time-centred kick. The force is 0 below Σ_vac: P8 resets those cells, and §1.3.3 keeps vacuum static.
None of this changes the contract (§2.4.4 says only "P4's momentum source … E is unchanged"); I state it here so it stays
visible.

## The oracle (R8)
- **Formula**: f = κΣF/c_sb (contract §2.4.4, SM-E22), written in the test from the contract: κ = κ₀(1 + X_H) (κ_dust = 0
  in physics.json, asserted), and Σ and X_H from the state the step starts from.
- **Where F comes from**: on the GPU, F is `side.flux` read at the step's start; in the twin, it is reference::heat's
  pinned pass on the state after the last step.
- **"The momentum gained"**: the forced step's momentum minus the force-free step's, both from the same state at the same
  Δt. The gas flow is the same in both, so the difference is the force alone.
- **Scene**: a 64 × 64 bounce world of hydrogen at rest, cold pressure off. The medium is Σ 1 at T 1; the slab (columns
  24–39, every row) is Σ 0.1 at T 10, so Π = 2 on both sides. P5 is pinned: F is computed every step and E is untouched.
  The light pushes the medium away from the slab, and the edge cells toward the world's edges.
- **Tolerance**: the plan's 1e-6 relative on the momentum gained, per half and per component (x by left/right halves,
  y by top/bottom), fixed before the first run. Σ, E and the species must equal the free step's bit for bit.
