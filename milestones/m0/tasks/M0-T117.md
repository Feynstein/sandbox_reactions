## Deviations and declared defaults

- G-HEAT2 (a)'s twin case does not call `assert_inside`: one Δt = 24 step reaches the edges (backward Euler's response
  falls as exp(−r/√(χΔt)), 3.5 cells); the case bounds the light leaving at 1e-6 of the pulse (measured 5.5e-8). The
  independent solve models the edge faces the same way (vacuum state beyond), so the 1e-10 comparison holds.
- The damping read as a per-cell clamp of each pass's T to [T/2, 2T] after u ≥ u_vac (contract (3): "a pass moves no
  cell's T by more than a factor 2 and keeps u ≥ u_vac") — cheap to reverse; a global step scale would need a reduction.
- F is computed at u_of(the final T) — the solved u when no clamp acts (the converged case).
- An added twin case beyond (a) and (b): the radiative slab's per-cell balance (G-STIFF (c)'s measure on a mild scene),
  because every other twin case on the radiative law reads sums only.
- `Pass::warning` now takes the step number (the contract's string names it); no caller used the old `warning()`.
