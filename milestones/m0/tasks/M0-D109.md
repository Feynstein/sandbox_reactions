## Why the formula and the twin
- ΔW of a mass change δm on m, φ linear in Σ: ½(m+δm)·K(m+δm) − ½m·Km = ½δm·(φ_m + φ_after) (K symmetric). P2 has φ_after;
  φ_m = φ_after,gas − φ_δ. With sinks the ledger's W is ½Σm(φ_gas + φ_sinks) (ledger.rs: the sinks' own mφ is not in it), so the
  booking is ½δm·(φ_after,gas − φ_δ) before `grav_sinks` plus ½δm·φ_after,total after it — W's own convention, kept.
- The ledger's W is ½·m_end·φ(start of step): it lags each step's own motion by half (the energy source is the mass fluxes' work at
  the step's φ, §0.5 [M0-D64]). On a hot paint's first step that is ≈ 0.5–1 % of its ΔW — not an edit's, no term books it. The
  scene's own step (start-up drift 9.4e-3 |W|/τ_dyn, over G-CONS with no edit) is what the twin takes out: D101's −0.89 beside a
  star was mostly that (the twin's step: −0.126; with the term the edit's step −0.131).
