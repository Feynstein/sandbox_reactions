## What exists now
- **crates/sr-physics/src/reference/react.rs.** `React::new` builds `Nfe` (the record's index, Fe and n, y_Fe = Z/A, A, a, Σ_g) when the
  enabled N_Fe has the closed form's shape — f ≡ 1 (no terms, no T_thr), one input of order 1, one output of Z = 0, a gate with p = 1;
  otherwise `None` and N_Fe sub-cycles as before. `burn` reads N_Fe's ω at the pass's start (T0, Y_e0): > 0 sets the cell's latch and
  `nfe_exact` steps it over the whole Δt; its heat q·Δm goes into E (unless T is pinned), `cell.de` and `booked[N]` at once; the sub-cycle
  loop then skips the record. `nfe_exact` evaluates the drop ΔY_e = u v φ/(1 + w φ), u = Y_e − b, v = Y_e − a_Y = y_Fe X_Fe, w the
  decaying one (u for c = b − a_Y ≥ 0, v for c < 0), φ = −expm1(−k|c|Δt)/|c| (kΔt when k|c|Δt = 0), k = A Σ^a/b — algebraically the
  report's Y − b = dE/(1 + d(1 − E)/(b − a_Y)), written so no exponential grows (Δt 10⁶) and no difference cancels. Δm = ΔY_e/y_Fe, capped
  at X_Fe. Landing: when the remaining w = w e^{−k|c|Δt}/(1 + wφ) is ≤ 4 ε b, a gate-limited cell is nudged (≤ 16 steps, each at least
  ε X_Fe, doubling) until Y_eΣ ≤ Σ_g as `Omega::g` tests it; an iron-limited one gives up all its iron (X_Fe = 0). The closed form is not a
  sub-step: `substeps`, `max_dx`, `max_dt_rel` count the sub-cycles alone.
- **crates/sr-engine/tests/gpu/burn.rs.** `cell::n_fe_steps_in_closed_form` (twin, no adapter) and its oracle `n_fe_march`: §1.6.1's law
  for X_Fe marched by classical RK4, 4,000 steps a Δt — not the closed form.
- **tests/plants/nfe-sub-cycled.patch** (new, in burn_cell): `nfe: nfe.filter(|_| false)` — N_Fe sub-cycled. Red on the case (its first

- **tests/plants/nfe-gate-sigma.patch** (neutrino's, rewritten): its twin hunk read the sub-cycle's ω, which no longer reads N_Fe; it now
