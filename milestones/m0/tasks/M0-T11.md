shares: unknown species, twice, outside (0, 1], not summing to 1 within 1e-12 (inputs and outputs, so mass is conserved per record) · `t_key` not one of the six

## Declared readings (the contract is silent; cheap to reverse)
1. **`a_coef` is a physics.json key** (A is a tuned constant there: `a_h_burn` … `a_n_fe`), resolved at parse. `rate.a` is the exponent of Σ, `orders` the n_i.
2. **A term's ν is a number or a physics.json key.** The fixed ones (H_burn 4 and 18, He_burn and C_alpha 40) are numbers; the six late records name `nu_c_burn` …
   `nu_s_burn`, so a scene's override of ν reaches the record. ν ≥ 4 is checked on the resolved value of every term (K7), not only the late records.
3. **T_thr = t_thr_factor × the first term's T_k**; `t_thr_factor` in (0, 1]. **N_Fe is `terms: []` (f ≡ 1) with `t_thr_factor: null`**; the pair must agree (terms ⇒ a
   threshold, no terms ⇒ null).
4. **`group` is one of H, He, C, Ne, O, Si, N** (§1.9.1's accumulators) — a new group is a code change there anyway.
5. **A stand-in record may not be enabled** at parse; the PLAN amendment that enables one edits this check with it (§1.6.4).
6. Shares must each lie in (0, 1], no species twice per side; orders > 0; q_ratio any finite number (N_Fe's is −1.14).
