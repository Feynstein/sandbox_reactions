# M0-D41 — rung_record.py reads a stub's handoff from its archived block

Box: linux-pc (laserax-ai). Rung: `rung_record.py now` → `model=claude-haiku-5-5 level=high plugin=installed` (the heading's
Haiku 5.5, high — no MISMATCH). Log: logs/M0-D41.log, logs/M0-D41.redarm.log, logs/M0-D41.report.json.

## Confirmed cause
`read_blocks()` merges a stub with its archived block into one text. `handoff_rung()` took the first `- Handoff` field, the
stub's one line, and never followed the stub's `- Full block: plan_archive.md § <id>`. A quote on the archived block's second
line was lost: M0-D5 and M0-T19 read `unknown`.

## What changed (tools/pb/rung_record.py)
- `FULL_RE` beside `QUOTE_RE`; `_field` and `_archived_field` (the archive's block through `P.find_blocks`) before `handoff_rung`.
- `handoff_rung(text, folder)`: the archived field's quote first, else the stub's quote, else the archived field's prose, else
  the stub's one line where the file or the block is missing. `build()` passes the plan's folder.
- Deviation (cheap default, declared): a stub's quote is kept where the archived block quotes nothing, so no row that quoted
  on its stub loses its rung.
- Selftest: stubs M9-T5 (archived quote on its second line; the stub's prose names another rung) and M9-T6 (archive holds no
  block); `HANDOFF_ARCHIVE`; `_handoff_rows` writes the archive; `_prose_first` takes `folder`; plant « a stub's archived block
  never followed, its one line read ».
