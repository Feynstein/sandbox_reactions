## The fix (the lead, 2026-11-19: «Take-back + carry (Recommended)», then «Proportional (Recommended)»)
- `hydro_push_sum` sums exactly (fixed point 2⁻⁸, 2⁻²⁸, 2⁻⁴⁸, `settle_fixed`) Σⁿ⁺¹ − Σⁿ over the box's free cells (past Σ 2²² the
  f32 difference, exact there) plus each boundary face's let-out mass (`hydro_edges` writes s·f[0] per slot into `push_net`, the
  f32 product it books `escaped`; 0 at a bounce wall), adds the carry, writes G and the free cells' mass M.
- `hydro_commit` gives G back as Σ += G·Σ/M to every free box cell (fractions, momentum, E as they are; skipped if |G| ≥ M/2).
- `hydro_settle` (new, one workgroup after the commit) sums exactly what f32 kept (committed Σ − Σⁿ⁺¹) and writes the carry
  G − kept into `push_net` word 10. The carry is `Resume::mass_carry`, the dump header's `resume.mass_carry` (written when ≠ 0,
  absent reads 0), read by `Step::resume_records` and restored by `Step::restore` via `Hydro::mass_carry` / `set_mass_carry`.
- First built as the ruled single-cell give-back (the densest free cell by Σⁿ⁺¹): it held the life, but `sod`'s two symmetry
  cases went red — identical rows 2.15 × 10⁻⁵ apart, the transposed run 3.02 × 10⁻⁵ (bound 1 × 10⁻⁵; 1.79 / 2.12 × 10⁻⁶
  before). Asked; proportional ships. `sod` reads its old values under it (its shares stay under half an ulp; the carry waits).
- Driver folding: the carry's f32 form g − ((σ + g) − σ) read 0 in every dump (M0-D199's finding again) — the carry is computed in
  the fixed-point words.
- Twin unchanged: in f64 it rounds nothing to give back. Files: crates/sr-engine/shaders/hydro.wgsl, src/step/hydro.rs,
  src/step/latch.rs, src/dump.rs, src/headless.rs (twin_dump's `mass_carry: 0.0`), tests/gpu/dump.rs (small_dump's literal,
