## The ruling
The lead (question tool, 2026-11-23), verbatim: **«Whole world, 1 px (Recommended)»** — as put: «The whole 800 × 800 world at
1 px a cell (800 × 800 px, 44 px spare); the mouse wheel zooms in as today (z up to 8), Home returns. Keeps "a star's life
squeezed to fit one screen" true and the Giant's envelope in view. Gives up 2-px cells at the default.» Not taken: «Open zoomed
at z = 2», «Taller default window».

(The «44 px spare» in the question came from reports/world_size.md § 2.1's 844 px; `legend::layout` subtracts 72 px under the world
from a 872 px centre, so the world fits with 0 px spare: 800 + 72 = 872 — the same vertical fit 600 × 400 had at s = 2. Only the
width changes, 1,200 → 800 px.)

  window uses.

  in cells × 4s, the brush at z = 8 in cells × 8s, the preset cells straddling the edges relative to w and h).
