  the problems and doesn't count it in a verdict.

  `handleError`, 1.95 s in `setBindGroup`; wasm itself < 1.5 s. The page waits on Chrome's GPU side; it doesn't compute.

  out by ≈ 3.5 s, each mapping a new `latch.readback` (7,713,484 B; Step::encode_frame makes one when every slot is busy) and an
