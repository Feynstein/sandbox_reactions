## What exists now
- `crates/sr-engine/src/watch.rs` — the Watch reads the embedded calibration (`embedded_calibration()`, as
  `Scene::with_embedded_masses` reads it). `Watch::new` and `Watch::restore` take the wisp cut from it: 1 % of
  `preset_mass_sb.sun` (31.367), `SUN_MASS_NOMINAL` (33.510) where none is embedded. `Watch::with_calibration(physics, w, t0, cal)`
  is the seam (`new` calls it with the embedded one). A private `Readouts` (MassScale, Predictor, AgeClock) is `None` with no
  calibration, or with anchors the translations refuse. Then all four keys are written null.
- summary.json's objects gain `mass_suns`, `predicted_ending` (fate ids: one for a sure call, the close call's two lighter first),
  `ending` (the ending id when `Predictor::readout` reads `Ended` at the last frame, from the Watch's own remnant holds and the
  last frame's τ_dyn; else null) and `age_years` (`AgeClock::age`, null before a stage has held).
- The age clock is updated in `Watch::update` every frame on each object's displayed stage. It is passed the frame's sim time as
  its "wall" time: its rate is never read in a headless run, and this keeps its samples bounded and the snapshot deterministic.
- `crates/sr-physics/src/observe/age.rs` — `AgeClock::snapshot` / `restore`. They carry each track's age, last sim time, shown
  stage, and per stage φ and x_start, every f64 an exact string. The rate's wall samples are not carried.
- `Star` (the progress tick's heaviest object) gains `id` and `age_years`. The tests read them per frame; `calibrate`'s line is
  unchanged.
- `crates/sr-engine/src/headless.rs` — `OBSERVERS_VERSION` 3. The Watch's snapshot gains `ages` (null from a binary with no
  calibration, restored as an empty clock). A version-2 snapshot is refused by `--continue` (exit 4) and not used as a checkpoint.
- `scenes/sun_13593.observers` (heat_stiff's M0-D156 fixture) upgraded in place: `"version":3` and `"watch":{"ages":[],…`, a
  two-spot textual edit, every other byte unchanged. The lead: «Upgrade it in place (Recommended)».
- `crates/sr-engine/tests/gpu/observe.rs` — 5 cases (9 → 14). The continued life's three runs are shared by its two cases, and
  the readouts' life by its two (`OnceLock`; the scope runs `--test-threads=1`).
- Plants `tests/plants/age-not-carried.patch` (the snapshot's `ages` null) and `tests/plants/wisp-cut-nominal.patch` (`sun_mass`
  returns the nominal), listed for `observe_run` in tools/pb/verify.json (`expected` 14).
- `crates/sr-engine/tests/gpu/calibrate_bisect.rs`: the «version 1» needle reads `OBSERVERS_VERSION`. A late fix, outside the
