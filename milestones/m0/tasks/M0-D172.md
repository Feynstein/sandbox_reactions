## (1) The rim's floor, per cell and per pass (Deliver (1))
**The probe.** The CPU twin (sr_physics::reference::Twin, f64) in a scratch copy, stepped from the GPU repro's dumps (`build/m0-d170/v1/dumps`
s_450992, s_451984 — M0-D170's Verify 1 run, the shipped tree) cropped to the 64 × 64 cells about the remnant (x 267–330, y 167–230; the
twin runs ≤ 128 × 128; outside is vacuum, gravity is free-space), the dump's mask, F and Δt resumed, every switch on (P3 and P7 are not in the
twin; neither acts here). Hooks (scratch only): the state after each pass, and in P4 per cell the four faces' dt·F, the gravity kick, the
face works and the radiation push. **The twin reproduces the GPU**: from s_450992, the last 1,000 steps floor 1.23 / radiated 9.45 per step
(0.130) against the GPU's 1.26 / 9.35 (0.135); from s_451984, 1.8–2.2 per step against the GPU's 1.8–3.2.

**The rim falls *into* the held wall, it does not flow out.** The 24 free cells round the held 5 × 5 less corners move at |u| 4.4–6.2
toward the core (left rim u > 0, right u < 0, top v > 0, bottom v < 0 — M0-D170's «outward» read the sign backwards), yet no mass moves:
the wall faces carry none by construction and the vacuum faces ~10⁻¹¹ per step (the MUSCL face toward vacuum is at Σ ≈ 0). The held wall's
HLL momentum flux (Π + 2Σu_n² + cΣu_n against the mirror) is what holds each edge cell up against Σg — an unresolved atmosphere, under one
cell, supported by a standing «phantom» infall.

**Per pass, the free rim's Σε_th per step (twin, 16 steps from s_451984):** P4 −11.06, P5 −6.37, P6 +0.74, P8's floor +2.02. Inside P4
the flux stage gives +1,400 (the wall's momentum flux decelerates the infall at constant E) and gravity's stage −1,526 (the kick re-adds the
kinetic energy). **The floor goes to the five thinnest cells only** — (302,201), (297,196), (296,201), (296,197), (301,202), Σ 0.47–0.58,
ε_th at T_floor/μ — each taking 0.2–0.6 per step; there P4 takes −1.5 to −2.7 per step and P5 *heats* (+1.2 to +2.8, conduction from the
hot inner corners), so neither the vacuum faces nor P5 take the rim under T_floor.

**Which term: §1.4.2's face work, at faces where cells carry momentum and no mass flux.** The face's energy-conserving split (`face_work`:
extra = Δt(q − ½(p_l + p_h))·Δφ, its residual shared by mass w = Σ_l/(Σ_l + Σ_h) after the pair impulse δ) books zero work for a pinned cell
— both of an edge cell's normal faces book 0 (the wall: still; the vacuum face: w → 0 gives 0.5Δt·p·d + rest = 0) while its kick Σg·Δt is
whole: (299,202), Σ 10, takes a kick −24.5 per step and E −2.7. A thin corner cell (302,201), Σ 0.45, which touches no held cell, takes
from the face to its pinned neighbour (301,201) (Σ 20, u 3.7 into the wall) its mass share of that pair's residual and the impulse:
−1.15 per step, −0.26 from its other free face, gravity work −1.41 per step against a thermal reserve Σε_th = 0.0125. The floor puts it
back. Its source: the held wall's standing infall (momentum without mass flux), not the wall's own flux (energy 0 by construction).
