## Built
- `shaders/sinks.wgsl`: `sink_update` ends after accretion. The motion is four new dispatches (six in P3, plus the latch):
  - `sink_pull`, over the box's 8 × 8 tiles (Indirect at ARGS_TILES). Each cell with Σ ≥ Σ_vac pulls each sink by
    G m d/(|d|² + r_acc²)^{3/2} (r_acc from the post-accretion mass). Each tile's partial per sink comes from a fixed tree
    and goes to `pull[PULL_TILES + tile × 16 + k]`.
  - `sink_kick_drift`, one workgroup: the tiles summed per sink in a fixed stride and a 256-wide tree, plus the pair's pull
    softened by the larger r_acc, the half-kick and the drift.
  - `sink_pull` again, at the drifted positions.
  - `sink_kick_merge`: the closing half-kick, then the merging as before.

  `bilinear`, `accel` and the `grav_g` binding are gone. Binding 10 is now the `pull` buffer: a head of 2 × 16 accelerations
  (each slot's a at the start and at the drifted position), then the tiles. `SinkParams` grows to 8 words with Σ_vac, so the
  motion's dispatches bind no EOS group.
- `step/sinks.rs`: the `sinks.pull` buffer, the six dispatches, and a `with_eos` switch per dispatch. `Sinks::new` loses its
  `g` parameter. `set_sinks` zeroes the pull head. `SinkReport::pulls` holds each slot's [a_start, a_drifted], before merging.
- `step/mod.rs`: the call no longer passes P2's gas field, and the doc lines are updated.
- `reference/sinks.rs`: `advance_pre_v7`, `step_pre_v7` and `bilinear` deleted (the carried flag); the module doc updated.
- `tests/gpu/sink.rs`:
  - Re-aimed at the new law: every `step_pre_v7` became `step`. `gpu_kick_drift_kick_and_merging_match_the_twin_in_the_gas`
    now uses `advance` on the cloud.
  - G-SINK's momentum check: the accretion and shared-cell cases take the kick out with `unkicked` (M v − M ½Δt(a0 + a1)
    from `pulls`) and stay at 1e-6.
  - New `gpu_the_sinks_pull_on_the_gas_is_equal_and_opposite_on_asymmetric_worlds`, on M0-D45's worlds. a_s is P3's closing
    pull and g_s comes in f64 from the sinks P3 left. The pull is also checked against the twin's `pull` at 1e-5, and the
    corner sink against the twin's `step` at 1e-5.
  - New `gpu_a_sink_outside_the_box_moves_as_the_twins`: 128 × 128, an 8 × 8 blob on cells 8–15, box (0, 0, 24, 24)

  - The GPU fall prints grid + sink momentum.
- `tests/plants/sink-pull-grid.patch`: the twin hunk is rewritten with an inline bilinear, because the twin's `bilinear` is

## Deviations (cheap to reverse)
1. The plant's GPU hunk is the gas's pull unsoftened, as the grid's field is, not "the shader's bilinear read back". The shader
   binds no g any more, so a read-back would mean a three-file plant (shader binding, sinks.rs binding list, mod.rs passing the
   gas field).
2. `same_sinks` in the accretion and formation cases is graded at 1e-5 ([M0-V7]'s grade for the sum, as the KDK cases already
   were). The masses stay at 1e-6 by their own asserts, and so does G-SINK's grid + sink momentum (kick taken out).
3. Five code files (Files: named three): `step/mod.rs` (the call) and `reference/sinks.rs` (the retirement).
4. A new readout, `SinkReport::pulls`, for the tests.
