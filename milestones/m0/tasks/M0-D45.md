  pair, 7.3e-16 for the sink accreting at the cloud's corner (13.2, 13.7) after accretion. Worst 7.3e-16 against the 1e-5 bound.

  the same order as V7's 0.39 % with no sink (P4's own), against 15.4 % under the old law.
