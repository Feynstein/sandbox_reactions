## f32 findings (measured on the RTX 5090)
1. A floor on h (first try: 1e-6 Δt_n, so f32's time-left always shrinks) breaks a runaway: a hot cell's sub-steps go
   to ~1e-9 Δt_n, the floor forces oversize steps through, and Δξ = h(ω + ∇ω·ΔX) cancels catastrophically (E fell to
   the floor, booked −6e8). Fixed by keeping the time done as a compensated two-float sum and the twin's own retry floor
   (1e-14 Δt_n): the free H_burn run then ends at T 3,614 (the twin 3,615).
2. Rounding each fraction every sub-step drifts it: the Ne_burn spectator Fe lost 1.4e-5 and H_burn's heat read 1.96e-5
   over q·Δm over 9,000 sub-steps (G-BURN's 1e-5 failed). Fixed by carrying each fraction as a two-float (rounded once at
   write-back) and renormalising by the compensated excess e = Σx − 1 (x − x e/(1 + e)); the heat sums are two-float too.
3. **The NVIDIA Vulkan compiler folds Knuth's two-sum away**: `add2((1, 0), 1e-8).y` read exactly 0, even with each step
   multiplied by a uniform 1.0. Only routing values through `bitcast<u32>` XOR a uniform 0 (`opaque`) kept the error terms
   (then the spectator holds 0.3 exactly). Any later compensated sum on this box needs the same.
4. ln f is capped at 60 (f ≈ 1e26): the runaway's f = (18,000/140)^40 ≈ 1e84 overflows f32; at the cap a sub-step is
   already far below Δt_n. MAX_ATTEMPTS 65,536 per cell per pass (the twin's 1,000,000), then `unfinished`.

  hydrogen, under f32's resolution at X = 1 — the GPU case lengthens the pass from the twin's rate).

  of 1 t.u.: P6 **42.4 ms on the Quadro** (0.10 ms all cold), 11.2 ms on the RTX 5090 (0.025 ms) — 196 serial sub-steps
  in one tile, ≈ 0.22 ms each on the Quadro (64 invocations busy, the Gauss in private memory).

  1.281 ms (box load 12–13 from other work; logs/M0-T46.headless-quadro.log — the first run's log name was taken by the

  (X_H moves ≤ 1e-6 by step 200) — P6's onset cost, not its loaded cost (the hot-core case above is).
