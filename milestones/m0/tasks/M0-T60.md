## Declared readings (cheap to reverse)
1. **Frame = 16 steps with `--until`, 256 without** (`headless::FRAME`, untuned). cold_disk's whole collapse is ~250 steps;
   stages hold 3 frames (§1.9.3), so 256-step frames never show `collapsing` (measured: the second frame is already past the
   collapse, and the tracker's identity — ≥ 25 % block overlap — gives the object a new id each frame while the cloud
   shrinks). Measured on the RTX 5090: chunk 16 → `collapsing` at step 49, chunk 64 → never within 3,000 steps. Cost of
   shorter frames, fill_world 800 steps: 2.5 s at 256, 3.1 s at 16, 4.6 s at 4. A run on `--steps` keeps 256 so no existing
   scope's numbers move.
2. `sim_time:<t>` stops at the first step past `t`: a chunk is at most half the time left at the next step's Δt (≥ 1 step), so
   the last steps are single; a Δt that doubled inside a chunk would overshoot — never seen. Asserted: the run one step
   shorter (`--steps n-1`) is under `t` (step 1355 at 5.002840; 1354 at 4.997223).
3. `stage:` / `event:` / `ending` are read on a frame's summary: the stop is at the frame's end, up to one frame past the
   step the condition first held. Asserted: the stop is on a frame boundary, and the run one frame shorter is `unmet`.
4. Event `step` and a stage's `from_step` are step indices from the scene (a dump's included), as `s_<step>.bin` is;
   summary.json's `steps` is the steps this run ran.
5. An object's `stage` is the displayed stage, `null` until one has held 3 frames; `objects` are the last frame's.
   `mass_suns`, `predicted_ending`, `ending`, `age_years` are left out until M0-T69, M0-T72, M0-T73.
6. Event `detail`: `{"mass_sb": m}` (ending: `{"stage", "held_t", "mass_sb"}`).
7. The ending's 10 t.u. are counted between frames' sim times (first frame showing the remnant to the frame in question).
8. The run's first step is a frame of its own (the ledger's start), so a stop is at step 1 + 16k.
