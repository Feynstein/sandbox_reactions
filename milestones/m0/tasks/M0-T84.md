# M0-T84 — The world in the window: the frame loop

Run on model=claude-sonnet-5-5 level=high (rung_record.py now, 2026-10-29), linux-pc (laserax-ai), lavapipe on a private Xvfb.

## What exists
- `crates/sr-app/src/desktop.rs`: `mod sim` — `Sim::new(&RenderState)` builds the engine on eframe's device (empty 600 × 400 world, every pass
  on, edge Leave, P0's edits, `Timing::Auto`, the Glow view, a second `State` for α, the cell image registered as a native egui texture,
  nearest filtering); `Sim::frame(now, frozen)` polls (reports → `TimeControl::on_report`, in order), plans (`plan_frame`), copies state →
  previous when steps > 0, `encode_frame`, `render`, submits once. The centre draws the image at s = max(1, ⌊min(w/W, h/H)⌋) physical px
  (2 in the default window), corner on a pixel; a 24 px status bar shows `status.line` live (§4.12 integers). The step counter stays as the
  stand-in readout until the panels. `device_descriptor` asks for TIMESTAMP_QUERY on native where the adapter has it.
- `capture.rs`: actions `preset` and `steps` (the real loop); `SR-CAPTURE-STATUS <id> <status line>` printed per step; `Driver::abort`.
- `tests/capture/world.json`, `tests/smoke.sh world` (desktop's 5th case), plant `tests/plants/world-not-drawn.patch`,
  `status-never-ready.patch` re-cut for the moved lines. verify.json: desktop/capture/web paths gain `crates/sr-physics/**` and the four
  `assets/*.json` (M0-V3's flag).

## Measured
- `verify.py desktop capture`: desktop 5/5, capture 2/2 GO. `--redarm desktop`: 6 plants, 6 red (557 s).
- Window centre of the empty world and its two corners are (5, 7, 13) = bg.space exactly: the cell image is not encoded twice (M0-V11's
  second duty; egui-wgpu reads its Rgba8Unorm textures as gamma bytes, the engine's image holds sRGB bytes, no view needed).
- At ×1 the Sun-like star is owed < 1 step a frame (Δt ≈ 0.0105 t.u. vs 1/60 per frame): 0.37–1 step a frame on lavapipe's 25 fps.
- An empty world steps with Δt = 0.447 (the floor gas's, M0-D96): after its first step TimeControl owes −0.447, so it steps once per ~27
  frames until a state arrives. A queued edit (a drop) lands at the next step, ≤ 0.45 s later at ×1 — T90 may want a step on an edit.

## Decisions (cheap to reverse)
- Steps per frame = mean steps of the frames that ran any in the last second (a mean over all frames reads 0 at ×1).
- α = 1 + owed/Δt_est when less than one step per frame is owed (the contract's `owed/Δt_est` is ≤ 0 under its own bound owed ≥ −Δt_est);
  1 otherwise. The app mirrors TimeControl's `owed` (no accessor exists).
- A capture's `steps` frames are forced: not planned, never cut by the latch, `on_report` gets them with steps_run 0 (Δt only).
- Plan said "desktop 3 cases"; the scope has had 4 since M0-D51/D99, so 5 now.

## Not done (named)
- Events into `on_event` (banner, auto slow-down) — M0-T86. The latch's cut frames still owe nothing.
- M0-D26's flag: the engine times a frame pass by pass; "time the step as one compute pass when no per-pass cost is asked" is the engine's
  `Timer`, outside this Deliver. Performance is never graded (M0-TJ2).
- fps at ×1 on a real GPU: not measured here (lavapipe only: 25 fps).
