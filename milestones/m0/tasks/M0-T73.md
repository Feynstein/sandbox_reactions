## Readings of the contract (declared, cheap to reverse)
- **Scope name** `age_clock`, not the plan's `age-clock` (R17: letters, digits, `_`).
- **Integrated, not recomputed.** §1.10.4's φ for a timed stage is "time in the stage ÷ calibrated duration at this mass"; read literally a mass change would move φ and jump the age. The clock sums Δt / D(M_now) frame by frame instead (equal at constant mass), so a mass jump alters the rate only (G-AGE).
- φ is clamped to [0, 1] per stage (a stage is worth its τ_s at most). Fuel added raises the stage's X_f,c,start; the burning of it counts again.
- A stage that ends is banked to the frame that ends it; a stage shown again goes on from where it stood (per-object, per-stage progress kept).
- `stage_durations_sb` log-log in mass and **held** beyond its pairs (the contract says log-log only); 10 t.u. where the stage has no entry. `collapsing` shares the cloud row; remnants flat 1e8 yr/t.u.
- Ages are per object id; a gone object is forgotten (a merge keeps the heavier id's age, as ids do). Sim time running back adds nothing.
