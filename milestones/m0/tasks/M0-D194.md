## Method
Each probe books its candidate's momentum into a ledger term the Sun-like life never uses (`escaped` in bounce, `held` — 0 over the
whole life), so the dynamics are untouched (every probe life is the base's bit for bit). Per-cell f32 booking (M0-D186's method) was
noise-bound here (±0.01 — the kick's per-cell terms are large and cancel); a TwoSum-compensated f32 sum over the box gave an
order artefact (K_y +13.6 against a grid drift of 0.004 — the driver may fold the TwoSum); the terms that stand are summed exactly,
in fixed-point i32 (2⁻²⁸; a coarser 2⁻²² quantum was ±4e-3 noisy) in one workgroup after `hydro_update` (`hydro_push_sum`'s slot).

## The fix (the lead, 2026-11-19: «Take-back, densest cell (Recommended)», «Book as escaped (Recommended)», «Measure first (Recommended)»)
- (a) `hydro_push_sum` sums, exactly (i32 two-word fixed point: 2⁻⁸ and 2⁻²⁸ units, carried), each step's net kick on the gas's
  own field (`Gravity::gas_field`): free cells' Δt·Σ_src·g_mid plus held cells' Δt·Σ_src·g_pull_mid — so the held core's pull and the
  sinks' pull, real exchanges booked `held` and by P3, cancel out of K — and finds the densest free cell; `hydro_commit` takes K back
  from that cell's momentum, E as it is. M0-D186's take-back spread over g (a = K/M ≈ 10⁻⁷) was under one ulp of g and lost; one
  cell's momentum holds it. The first version summed the total field over the free cells only: `ledger`'s held disk read 3.98 of
  Σm|u| (band 1e-3) — the free cells' kick from the held core is real; with the held cells' g_pull added, 2.96e-5.
- (b) `hydro_edges` books bounce's wall flux into `escaped` (its mass, energy and species 0: the walls' pressure impulse alone, the
  box's recoil).
- Twin alike (sr_physics::reference::hydro: the net on `own` fields, the same densest cell; bounce books its walls). Contract §0.5
  [M0-D194], §1.4.2, §2.8, §2.9 amended in place; Superseded mirrored (R19).
