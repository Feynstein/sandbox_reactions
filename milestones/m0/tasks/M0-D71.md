## Cause, as measured here
The remnant is unresolved — one or two cells (contract §2.7: R₉₀ under one cell from M ≈ 0.5 M_ch). A per-axis profile is exact only in
1D (the strip settles); in 2D no discrete equilibrium satisfies both axes, so a residual flow remains. Under RC the 1-cell core becomes a
pair oscillating against each other (v ±0.17), both cells drawing the floor; with HLLC's low dissipation the oscillation grows (KE ×180
over steps 3,000–10,000). Mass moved between cold cells whose ε_cold differs strongly leaves thermal deficits that P8 refills, so any
residual motion of a cold core is paid by the floor. H balances the barotropic core but T-floor gas is not isentropic, and the pump moves
onto the centre cell. No screened reconstruction or solver holds a sub-cell remnant at rest.
