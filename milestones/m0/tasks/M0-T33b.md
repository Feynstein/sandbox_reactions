  as the floor state (Σ_floor, at rest, T_floor); a cell and its four neighbours all below Σ_vac are left untouched.

## Deviations and decisions
1. **The scene's world is 400 × 64, not 400 × 4.** §2.8 and `WorldConfig` refuse a side under 64 cells (and `every_scene_file_parses`
   loads every file in scenes/), so the file fills 64 identical rows; the twin's 400 × 4 strip — G-SOD's literal case — is its
   first four rows (`State::rows`). M0-T34's GPU case runs the 400 × 64 scene (flag filed). Contract G-SOD's wording is unchanged
   (§5.1 is not yet FROZEN: no GO red-arm record before this task); M0-V5 or a PLAN task may want to word it "a 400 × 4 strip on the
   twin, 64 identical rows in the scene".
2. **The twin takes no gravity or radiation source** (M0-T36, M0-T43), nor the leave edge (M0-T35): `dt` takes g for the Δt term only.
3. The test's expected wall momentum uses the planes' f32 pressures (0.1 is stored as 0.100000001490116); an exact 0.1 read 1.7e-9 off.
