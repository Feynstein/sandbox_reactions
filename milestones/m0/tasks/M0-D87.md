## What exists now
- **crates/sr-engine/shaders/react.wgsl.** A record flagged `EXACT` (8) is N_Fe in its closed form's shape. `react_cells` reads its
  rate once at the pass's start — `A Σ^a X_Fe g(Y_eΣ)` through `rate_g` — and > 0 sets `latched`. `nfe_exact` then steps it over the
  whole Δt_n before the sub-cycles: the twin's ΔY_e = u v φ/(1 + w φ) in f32, with a_Y summed over every species but iron (not Y_e −
  y_Fe X_Fe, which cancels for pure iron) and φ = (1 − e^{−k|c|Δt})/|c|. `one_minus_exp` is −expm1(−x), which WGSL lacks: below 0.7
  it is a ten-term Horner series, above it 1 − exp(−x). Landing: when the gap left is ≤ 4 f32 ε b, a gate-limited cell is nudged
  (≤ 16 times, from ε X_Fe, doubling) until `rate_g` reads 0, so the next pass's rate is 0. An iron-limited cell gives up all its iron.
  The heat q Δm goes into e_tot (unless pinned), de_sum and group N at once. `rates` gives EXACT records `rec_live = false`, so the
  sub-cycles skip N_Fe (iron the sub-cycles make waits for the next pass, as on the twin). The write-back runs when `substeps > 0 ||
  moved`. The report stays 8 words: the closed form counts as no sub-step.
- **crates/sr-engine/src/step/react.rs.** `exact(rec, species)`: the twin's `Nfe` predicate (f ≡ 1, one input of order 1, one output
  of Z = 0 — Z read from `Elements::shipped()`, whose Z are §1.5's fixed table — and a gate with p = 1) sets the `EXACT` flag.
- **crates/sr-engine/tests/gpu/burn.rs.** `cell::gpu_n_fe_steps_in_closed_form`: D86's four cells on the GPU, with the twin stepped
  beside each.
- **crates/sr-engine/tests/gpu/neutrino.rs.** `gpu_latch_is_set_by_a_rate_that_closes_within_the_step` reads "at the gate": Fe fell
  within 1e-6 of what closes it, Y_eΣ ≤ Σ_N (1 + f32 ε) in the f64 view, the latch set, and a second pass sets nothing.
- **Plants.** `tests/plants/nfe-gpu-sub-cycled.patch` (new, burn_cell's): the host never flags EXACT. `nfe-latch-last` (rewritten,
  shader only): the latch reads N_Fe's rate after the closed form, i.e. at the pass's end. `nfe-gate-sigma`: its shader hunk moved to
  the pass-start read (`rate_g(r, sigma)`); the twin hunk is unchanged.
- **crates/sr-engine/tests/gpu/calibrate_bisect.rs** (M0-D80's case, at the lead's word, 2026-10-23 — «Re-baseline here
  (Recommended)»): `a_life_steps_one_at_a_time_through_its_core_collapse` now asserts `stretch >= 20` (was 30) and its doc says "some
  22 steps". At §1.6.1's rate the iron block's gate closes after 22 one-step frames (steps 2–23), not ~60. The cut at 20 still falls
  inside the stretch.
- **tools/pb/verify.json**: burn_cell expected 17 → 18, the plant and its path, desc; neutrino's desc (the closing-gate case, the
  re-aimed plants); calibrate_bisect's desc (22 one-step frames). **docs/agent/testing.md**: the burn_cell, neutrino and
