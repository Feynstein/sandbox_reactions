# M0-T76 — Player edits on the GPU (P0)

Model/level: claude-sonnet-5-5, high (`rung_record.py now`, 2026-10-28). Box: linux-pc (laserax-ai), RTX 5090 — not the server (R18, below).

## What exists
- `crates/sr-engine/src/step/edit.rs` — `Edit` {Paint, Erase, Heat, Cool, DropPreset(Cloud), Clear}, `EditError` {NoRoom → `presets.no_room`, Invalid, QueueFull, NotBuilt}, `Edits` (queue + patch buffers + 3 dispatches), `Step::with_edits(...)`, `Step::queue_edit(queue, &Edit)`, `Edit::drop_preset(preset, mass, cell, world, physics, elements)`, `Edit::restarts_ledger()`.
- `crates/sr-engine/shaders/edit.wgsl` — `edit_gate` (1 inv.: sizes `edit_cells` through the box's gate buffer, sets the box edit flag when a paint/drop lands outside the box), `edit_cells` (one inv. per cell, queue walked in order), `edit_finish` (queue emptied, sinks removed on Clear).
- `crates/sr-engine/src/scene.rs` — `preset_cloud` / `Cloud` / `CloudError`: the same cloud `Scene::preset_mass` loads, as a Σ patch + T + composition; `NoRoom` when the world clips any cell of it.
- accessors: `BoxFit::gate`, `Sinks::state_buffer`; `Step.edits` field and `pub mod edit` in step/mod.rs.
- tests/gpu/edit.rs (10 cases), scope `edits` in tools/pb/verify.json (expected 10), tests/plants/edit-ignored.patch.

## Readings declared
- Opt-in: `Step::with_edits` adds P0's dispatches to a built step; `Step::new` and its siblings keep their pass lists (state.rs / twin.rs assert P0 empty). Headless and the app do not call it yet.
- Dispatches: `edit_gate` and `edit_finish` take the latch's fixed 1×1×1 slot, `edit_cells` the box gate the latch already zeroes — the latch's "exactly two indirect buffers" assertion still holds. Cost: 3 small dispatches every step with edits built, unmeasured.
- Deposit (paint, drop): Σε_th is added to; the cold energy follows the new Σ and mixed composition; the momentum density is unchanged (kinetic energy follows it on the larger Σ). Booked = the stored change in E and Σ per cell, so the ledger balances to the bit apart from f32 rounding of the subtraction (< 1e-6 of the field's total, measured exact to the test's resolution).
- Erase and Clear write the floor state of pure hydrogen (M0-T3's default composition). Heat and Cool act on non-vacuum cells only (Σ ≥ Σ_vac) and a cool never raises ε_th; both floor at T_floor.
- Box: the flag is set only by a paint or a drop whose rectangle is not inside the active box; erase/heat/cool/clear leave the periodic re-fit to shrink it.
- Queue: 8 edits and 300 × 300 patch cells per step; a ninth edit is `QueueFull`. A second `queue_edit` before a step joins the first when no step has been encoded since (`Step::next`).
- Clear: GPU side zeroes cells and the sinks' count; the caller restarts its `Ledger` (`Edit::restarts_ledger`). Not wired.

## Measured, not acted on
- P1's Δt for the step that applies a paint is the previous step's (P9 made it): a paint into a box that was all cold vacuum raises c up to ≈ 2.8 (paint_t = 2) where the old Δt assumed ≈ 0.45 — a CFL of up to ~2.5 for that one step. Not reproduced (the tests run no P4); a hot box makes it moot. If a run shows it: P0 could re-run P9's reduction after the edits.
- Step 0: edits queued before the first step apply after the initial Δt reduction, so step 0's Δt is the pre-edit state's.
