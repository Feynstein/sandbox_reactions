## The Adversarial line — the latch zeroes the box re-fit's dispatch (code and step-15 case, win-laptop)
**From the shader.** Under a frame every dispatch reads its size from a buffer the controller can zero: `Latch::new` builds one
slot per (size, period) from every pass, the guard (period 64) and **the re-fit (`box_cells`, `box_fit`: period 16)**, and
asserts the step reads no indirect buffer but the box's sizes and P0's gate. When an armed set fires, `latch_check`'s
`zero_sizes` zeroes **every** slot (`3 × arrayLength(&sizes)` words — the re-fit's included), the box's sizes and P0's gate
(saved for `latch_end`), and every later `latch_check` returns before `open_sizes`, so nothing reopens a slot; `dt_advance`
is zeroed with the rest, so the GPU's step index stops where the fire left it. **Conforms.**

**The step-15 case, by mutation** (scratch copy, `bash tests/gpu.sh latch physics`, RTX 4080 Laptop):
| Mutant | k = 1, 15, 40 (the case) | k = 15 alone |
|---|---|---|
| M-a — `zero_sizes` spares the period-16 slots (the controller « forgets » the re-fit) | **killed at k = 1**: 39 box-sized dispatches after the fire, 1 wanted | survives (5/5 GO) |
| M-b — the re-fit recorded outside the latch, on the CPU's count (`record_dispatches` when n ≡ 0 mod 16) | killed | **killed**: 38 box-sized dispatches, 15 wanted; the edit case too (`fits` 5 against 3) |
So the scope kills both, but not by the case the block names for it: k = 15 catches a re-fit beyond the controller's reach
(M-b); a controller that spares the re-fit's own slot (M-a) is caught by k = 1, because the slots are opened one step ahead
and stay as the fire left them — the re-fit's was already closed for index 15, and the frozen index never reaches 16. The
module keeps both cases today; flagged to M0-T111 so the contract case does not replace them.
