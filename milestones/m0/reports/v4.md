## The Adversarial line — gravity graded only by its twin, the same mistake twice
**From the code.** `grav::force::disk_error` measures |Δg|/|g| against −(π²GΣc/2a)·r cell by cell; `selfforce::net_and_scale`
sums Σ m·g and Σ m|g|; the GPU cases (`gpu_disk`, `gpu_asym_blob_has_no_net_self_force`) call the same functions on the
GPU's g read back over the fitted box, and `against_twin` (G-REF's 1e-3) is a second arm, not the grade. The only shared
input is the kernel: the GPU's K̂ table is built from the twin's `grav::kernel` (step/grav.rs `quarter`), so a wrong K is
the same mistake twice, and only the oracles and the kernel's own assertion can see it.

**By plant and mutant** (scratch copy, `bash tests/gpu.sh grav physics`, RTX 4080 Laptop; 11 shipped cases):
